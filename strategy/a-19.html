A-19 Double

Definition

A-19 is a ratio-style option hedge: buy 1 lot and sell 2 lots. It is intended for low-to-moderate VIX, rangebound or resistance-area markets where sold premium can decay while the bought leg protects part of the tail move.

Base Structure

Bullish CE-side example:

Leg Action
Nearer/lower CE strike Buy 1 lot
Higher CE strike Sell 2 lots
Optional far OTM weekly CE Buy 1 lot for margin/tail protection

PE side is possible but needs more adjustment skill. For indexes, the source prefers CE side.

Entry Conditions

  • VIX should be low or stable; avoid VIX above 23.
  • Avoid when VIX is falling from a convergence point and market can rally steadily.
  • Prefer rangebound market near major resistance for CE-side structures.
  • Do not create CE ratio near support.
  • Higher buy premium relative to sell premium is acceptable; defined risk is the debit.
  • Know the risk zone and expiry breakeven before entry.

Strike Selection

  • Buy around a meaningful strike based on the view.
  • Sell strike can be approximated by buy strike + buy premium, rounded to nearest strike.
  • Example: buy 16000 CE at 229.95, sell two 16300 CE around 109.55.
  • Optional weekly far OTM buy reduces margin and helps with large overnight gaps.

Profit Logic

  • Downside: loss should be limited to debit if no additional shorts are added.
  • Rangebound: sold premium decay is the main profit engine.
  • Moderate upside: bought leg gains and sold legs decay/offset.
  • Strong upside beyond breakeven: loss can begin; adjustment required.

Adjustment Rules

  • Periodically book profit from the sell side.
  • If market falls, sell additional far OTM calls above the buying strike; never sell below the buying strike.
  • If market jumps sharply to a new zone, exit both legs and rebuild near the new level.
  • If upside band blast occurs, exit ratio and convert to bull call spread.
  • Keep margin reserved for adjustment.

Exit Rules

  • Book stock spread profit around 40-45% of maximum profit.
  • Far OTM stock spread may hold to 60-70% of maximum profit.
  • Index spread may hold to 60-70% of maximum profit.
  • Exit/rebuild if market reaches a new view zone quickly.
  • Exit if the structure violates the planned risk zone.

Backtest Specification

Minimum test inputs: - Underlying: Nifty, Bank Nifty, or liquid stock options. - Entry date/time relative to monthly expiry. - VIX filter: exclude VIX > 23. - Market regime filter: rangebound near resistance, or define by Bollinger/VWAP/20 SMA. - Strikes: one bought, two sold, optional far OTM weekly buy. - Entry price: actual option premium at signal time. - Exit: 40%, 60%, 70% of max profit variants; expiry hold variant. - Adjustment variants: no adjustment, book sell-side profit, rebuild on breakout.

Cross-Check vs Rita's BB Breakout V2

  • A-19 is not a breakout-following strategy; it is mostly range/resistance premium decay.
  • If BB breakout v2 confirms a strong band blast, A-19 ratio should be avoided or converted to directional spread.
  • A-19 can be backtested separately as an options payoff/regime strategy before mixing with BB scanner signals.
