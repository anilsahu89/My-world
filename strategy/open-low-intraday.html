Open=Low Intraday Strategy (NSE 200)

Definition

Every morning at 9:30, scan NSE 200 stocks. If a stock's Open price = its Low (stock opened at the day's lowest point and never went below), buy ₹10,000 worth. Hold until 1 PM or SL hit (close below the first 15-min candle low).

Core Philosophy

Open=Low means the stock opened at the absolute bottom of the day. Every tick after the open is ABOVE the entry price. This is a strong bullish signal — the entire market day is working in your favor from the first second.

⚠️ Data limitation: Backtested with daily OHLCV (no intraday data). SL modeled as "daily Low goes 0.5% below Open." Real 15-min SL will perform slightly differently. See caveats.

The Volume Discovery

Volume is the single most important filter. Without it, win rate is 47.5% (coin flip). With Vol ≥ 1.5× average, win rate jumps to 59.2% and PF to 4.25.

Volume confirms that smart money is participating in the Open=Low move. Without volume, it's just noise.

Rules

Entry (9:30 AM scan)

  1. Stock must be in NSE 200 universe
  2. Open = Low EXACTLY — difference between Open and Low must be ≤ ₹0.10 (10 paisa) - This is a STRICT rule: Open and Low must be essentially identical - No percentage tolerance — fixed 10 paisa maximum gap - Filters out stocks that "opened near low" but actually dipped intraday
  3. Volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-day average volume (MANDATORY — this is the edge)
  4. Price ≥ ₹50
  5. Buy ₹10,000 worth (delivery/cash, not margin)

Stop Loss

  • SL = 0.5% below the open price (first 15-min candle low proxy)
  • If a 15-min candle CLOSES below this level → exit immediately
  • In live trading: use the actual first 15-min candle's low as SL

Exit

  • 1:00 PM — exit all positions regardless of P&L
  • OR SL hit — whichever comes first
  • Don't hold overnight — this is purely intraday

Position Sizing

  • ₹10,000 per stock (fixed)
  • Max 3-5 stocks per day (pick the highest volume ratio signals)
  • Total daily capital at risk: ₹30,000-50,000

How to check volume at 9:30

  1. Compare today's first 15-min volume to the 20-day average daily volume ÷ 25 (approximate per-candle volume)
  2. If first 15-min volume > 1.5× expected → volume confirmed, trade it
  3. If volume is normal or low → skip, even if Open=Low

Honest Caveats

  1. Daily data proxy: The backtest uses daily OHLCV, not 15-min candles. The real SL (15-min close below first candle low) will perform slightly differently. The 1.0% OL tolerance is a proxy for "open near low" — real Open=Low at 9:30 is more precise.
  2. Exit price approximation: We use (Open+Close)/2 as a proxy for 1 PM exit. Real 1 PM price could be higher or lower.
  3. No costs included: Add brokerage (~₹20/trade) + STT + slippage. On 7,242 trades × ₹20 = ₹144K drag.
  4. In-sample: Filters found on same data they're tested on. Expect live WR ~55% (not 59%).
  5. High trade frequency: Baseline generates 90 trades/day across NSE 200. Even with Vol 1.5× filter, that's ~14/day. You MUST cap at 3-5/day for practical execution.
