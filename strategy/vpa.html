Strategy: VPA (Volume Price Analysis)

⚠️ Status: Rule defined, NOT yet backtested. Per CLAUDE.md hard rule, treat all performance claims below as hypotheses to test, not validated results. A dedicated backtester is a pending task.

Philosophy

Trade the "Volume is King" principle from Vpa: rising volume that price has not yet confirmed = smart money accumulating/distributing. Enter on the 3-candle confirmation pattern, not on a single bar.

This is the standalone tradeable form of VPA. VPA is also used as a filter inside BB Trap and the pending GFS strategy — see Vpa "How VPA is Used."

Entry Rules

Long setup

# Rule
1 Day 1: red candle (close < open) AND falling volume (vol[t-1] < vol[t-2]) — sellers exhausting
2 Day 2: volume rises ≥ 1.5× Day 1 (vol[t] ≥ 1.5 × vol[t-1]) AND Day 2 closes green (close > open)
3 Both rules true → enter long at Day 2 close or Day 3 open

Short setup (mirror)

# Rule
1 Day 1: green candle (close > open) AND falling volume — buyers exhausting
2 Day 2: volume rises ≥ 1.5× Day 1 AND Day 2 closes red (close < open)
3 Both rules true → enter short at Day 2 close or Day 3 open

Filters & Context (optional, for higher confidence)

  • Volume multiple: ≥1.5× is the baseline; ≥2.0× = strong signal (lifts conviction, fewer trades).
  • BB context (boosts win rate, untested):
  • Long setup near/under lower Bollinger Band = Moh Maya variant → stronger — see Moh Maya
  • Short setup near/over upper BB = BB Trap variant → stronger — see Bb Trap, Bb Trap Positional
  • Avoid: very low-volume stocks (smart-money footprint unreadable); avoid the first/last 15 min on intraday TFs (volume noise).

Stop Loss

  • Long: SL = Day 1 low (the exhaustion candle's low). If price closes below Day 1 low, the accumulation thesis is wrong.
  • Short: SL = Day 1 high.

Target / Exit

  • Fixed target: 1.5–2× the risk distance (R:R 1.5–2.0).
  • Trail alternative (NK convention): trail above 5 SMA on the entry timeframe — exit when price closes below 5 SMA (longs) / above 5 SMA (shorts). See Moving Average.
  • Time stop: if target not hit within ~5 candles of the entry timeframe, exit at market (avoid dead trades).

Timeframe

Works on any TF (Rita's requirement). Defaults: - Swing: daily candles (hold 2–10 trading days) - Intraday: 15-min candles (hold intra-session) - Positional bias: weekly/monthly for larger moves

Risk per Trade

  • Risk ≤ 1–2% of capital per position (consistent with the rest of Rita's system).
  • One position per symbol at a time; no pyramiding until backtested.

Universe

  • NSE 200 (use data/nse200_symbols.csv) for swing trades.
  • Nifty 500 for wider scan if backtest confirms edge.
  • Skip penny stocks (close < ₹50) and ultra-low volume (avg vol < 100,000).

Backtest Spec (pending)

When the VPA backtester is built, test: 1. Baseline win rate of the 3-candle long pattern (Day1 red+red → Day2 green vol≥1.5× + green close). 2. Baseline win rate of the short mirror. 3. Volume multiple sweep: 1.5× / 2.0× / 2.5× — win rate vs trade count trade-off. 4. BB context value: does adding the "near lower BB" filter (long) materially lift WR? 5. Trail vs fixed target: which exit rule produces higher PF? 6. Timeframe sweep: daily vs 15-min consistency. 7. Costs: include brokerage + slippage (₹0.05–0.10/side) in the final P&L.

Hard Rules (from CLAUDE.md)

  • Do not present this strategy as proven until the backtest spec above is run.
  • Volume is the primary signal — never enter on candle color alone.
  • Always pair VPA entry with the SL rule; VPA reduces risk, it does not eliminate it.
