Nifty Daily Theta — Paper Trade Tracking

Overview

Paper trading the Nifty Daily Theta strategy — selling hedged credit spreads Mon-Wed.

Rules

  • Entry: Mon, Tue, Wed after 1 PM
  • VIX: < 22
  • Direction: Above 20 SMA → Bull Put | Below → Bear Call
  • Sell: 300 OTM | Buy: 400 OTM (100pt hedge)
  • Expiry: Nearest 1-7 DTE
  • Exit: 50% profit OR strike hit (SL) OR expiry
  • Max 1 open position at a time
