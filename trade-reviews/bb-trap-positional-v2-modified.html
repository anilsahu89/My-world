Final Recommended Strategy (v2 + Optimal Filters)

SHORT SETUP (the primary edge)

1. Primary Candle (t-2): entire candle above upper BB (low > upper_bb)
2. Alert Candle (t-1): UPPER WICK ≥ 50% of range (rejection signal)
3. Volume: vol_a ≥ 1.5× vol_p
4. RSI at signal: > 70
5. Entry: short at current day close
6. SL: entry + (primary_range × 0.30)  [30% of primary range above entry]
7. Target: entry − (primary_range × 0.80)  [80% of primary range below entry]
8. Time stop: 10 trading days

Expected performance (with Vol1.5 + RSI filters): - PF: ~2.0-2.1 - Win rate: ~66% - R:R: 2.7× - Frequency: ~47 trades/year (~4/month)

LONG SETUP (secondary, marginal edge)

1. Primary Candle (t-2): entire candle below lower BB (high < lower_bb)
2. Alert Candle (t-1): LOWER WICK ≥ 50% of range (rejection signal)
3. Volume: vol_a ≥ 1.5× vol_p  (mandatory)
4. Entry: long at current day close
5. SL: entry − (primary_range × 0.50)
6. Target: entry + (primary_range × 1.00)
7. Time stop: 10 trading days

Expected performance: - PF: ~1.06 (barely profitable — use with caution) - Win rate: ~43% - R:R: 2.0× - Frequency: ~24 trades/year (~2/month)

What made the difference

  1. Rejection wick (biggest fix) — went from identifying trends to identifying actual rejections. This alone lifted win rate from 39% to 59%.
  2. Fixed target at 80% instead of 20 SMA — target hit rate jumped from 6% to 50%
  3. Tighter SL (30%) — kept R:R positive at 2.7×
  4. Vol ≥ 1.5× + RSI > 70 filters — lifted PF from 1.41 to 2.10

Honest Caveats

  1. In-sample optimization — grid search found 80%/30% on the same data it's tested on. Expect PF to regress ~20-30% live (target PF ~1.5-1.6 live, still tradeable)
  2. Costs not included — add ~₹100-150/trade for brokerage + slippage. On 327 trades = ₹33-49K drag. Net would be ₹76-92K instead of ₹125K.
  3. Long side is marginal — PF 1.06 is barely above breakeven. Only trade longs with the strictest filters.
  4. Signal frequency dropped — v2 short signals (327) are 65% fewer than v1 (933). But each trade is higher quality.
  5. Only 2.5 years of data — need to validate on different market regimes.
