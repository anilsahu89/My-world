Honest Interpretation

The bad news first

This strategy does NOT work as specified. The core problem:

  1. T1 hit rate is only 5-6% — the target is the 20 SMA, but price only reaches it ~1 in 17 trades. The strategy is supposed to "ride to 20 SMA" but it almost never gets there.

  2. 60-69% of trades hit the stop loss — the SL (alert candle extreme) is too tight. Most trades get stopped out before any mean reversion happens.

  3. LONGS lose money (PF 0.89, net −₹42K). The long side has no edge at all.

  4. SHORTS barely profit (PF 1.13) — barely above breakeven, and this is before costs. After brokerage + slippage, shorts would likely be net negative too.

Why it fails

The rules require: - Primary candle ENTIRELY outside BB (no wick touching) — this is extremely rare and signals such an extreme deviation that... - Alert candle closes EVEN FURTHER beyond primary candle's extreme — this means price is TRENDING HARD in the wrong direction, not reverting

The setup identifies strong trends, then bets on reversal. That's why 60-69% hit SL — the trend continues, the reversal never comes.

The target (20 SMA) is too far away because by the time you enter (after 2 candles of trending), the 20 SMA has moved and the distance is large.

Key Findings

Finding 1: LONGS only work with volume filter ≥ 1.5x

Adding vol_a ≥ 1.5 × vol_p flips the long side from −₹42K loss to +₹48K profit (PF 0.89 → 1.23). This is the single most impactful filter for longs.

Finding 2: SHORTS are barely profitable and hard to improve

The baseline short is marginally profitable (PF 1.13). The best single filter is RSI > 70 (PF 1.17, Net ₹2.45L), but the improvement is modest. Even the best filter combo doesn't push PF above 1.2.

Finding 3: Higher volume multiples HURT shorts

Counterintuitively, requiring vol ≥ 1.5x on shorts REDUCES profitability (PF 1.13 → 1.03). High-volume shorts tend to be strong breakdowns that continue trending. On longs, the opposite — high volume confirms the selling climax and reversal.

Finding 4: The T1 target (20 SMA) is almost never reached

Only 5-6% of trades reach 20 SMA. This means the exit strategy is fundamentally broken. The real exit is either SL (60-69%) or time stop (27-37%). The strategy as designed is a slow bleed.

Recommendations — How to Actually Make This Work

Problem 1: The entry is too late (2 candles of confirmation = trend already running)

Fix: Instead of requiring the alert candle to close BEYOND the primary candle's extreme (which means the trend is accelerating), look for the alert candle to REJECT the extreme:

Modified Rule 2: Alert candle forms a long wick in the opposite direction (rejection), not a close beyond the extreme. This catches the actual trap moment.

Problem 2: The SL is too tight

Fix: Use the primary candle's extreme as SL (further away) instead of the alert candle's extreme. Or use an ATR-based SL (2× ATR).

Problem 3: The target is too far

Fix: Instead of targeting the 20 SMA (which is rarely reached), target a fixed % reversion or the nearest VWAP. Or use a trailing exit once the trade is in profit.

Problem 4: Longs need volume, shorts need RSI

Fix: Apply different filters per direction: - SHORTS: RSI > 70 (PF 1.17, ₹2.45L net) — the best filter - LONGS: Volume ≥ 1.5× (PF 1.23, +₹48K net) — flips to profit

Recommended Modified Strategy (for next backtest)

SHORT setup:
  1. Primary candle: entire candle above upper BB (low > upper BB)
  2. Alert candle: LONG UPPER WICK (wick > 50% of range) — REJECTION, not continuation
  3. Volume: vol_a > vol_p
  4. RSI at signal: > 70
  5. Entry: short at alert candle close
  6. SL: primary candle HIGH (not alert candle high)
  7. Target: 50% of the primary candle range (fixed target, not 20 SMA)

LONG setup:
  1. Primary candle: entire candle below lower BB (high < lower BB)
  2. Alert candle: LONG LOWER WICK (wick > 50% of range) — REJECTION
  3. Volume: vol_a >= 1.5× vol_p  (mandatory, flips to profit)
  4. Entry: long at alert candle close
  5. SL: primary candle LOW (not alert candle low)
  6. Target: 50% of the primary candle range

Why these changes should help

  1. Rejection wick = price tried to push further but failed = actual trap signal (not trend continuation)
  2. Primary candle SL = wider stop, lets the trade breathe before reversal
  3. Fixed % target = achievable target (50% reversion is far more common than full return to 20 SMA)
  4. Direction-specific filters = each side gets what the data says works
