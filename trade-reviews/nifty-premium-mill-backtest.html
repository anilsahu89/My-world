Key Findings

Finding 1: Tuesday entry is dramatically better than Monday

Entry DayWin%PFNet P&L
Monday (baseline)64.4%1.05₹3,041
Tuesday76.0%1.52₹27,139

Tuesday entry nearly 9x the P&L. Why? Less time to expiry means more theta decay per day. Monday entries have 3-4 days to expiry; Tuesday entries have 2-3 days. The theta curve is steeper closer to expiry.

Finding 2: Wider OTM = higher win rate but lower premium

OTM DistanceWin%PFAvg Credit
50 pts OTM52.1%1.02₹18.4
100 pts (baseline)64.4%1.05₹27.7
150 pts69.9%1.15₹22.6
200 pts79.5%1.39₹18.3

200 OTM gives 79.5% win rate but only ₹18 premium. The sweet spot is 200 OTM with 100pt spread width — high win rate with manageable premium.

Finding 3: VIX 12-16 is the goldilocks zone

Low VIX (12-16) is best. This makes sense: low VIX = stable markets = OTM spreads expire worthless more often. High VIX (>20) has fat premiums but high gap risk.

The May 2025 problem

May 2025 lost ₹10,605 — wiping out 3 months of gains. This was likely a sharp directional move that hit multiple strikes. This is the strategy's core vulnerability: a sudden trend change can hit SL on several spreads at once.

Fix: The Tuesday + VIX 12-16 filter would have skipped many of these trades (if VIX spiked above 16 during the event).

Honest Assessment

What works ✅

  1. Win rate 64-87% is real and achievable — OTM credit spreads have inherent positive expectancy when theta works in your favor
  2. Tuesday entry is clearly better — steeper theta curve near expiry
  3. VIX 12-16 zone filters out the high-gap-risk environments
  4. 50% profit booking is optimal — don't get greedy, don't exit too early

What doesn't work ❌

  1. Base PF is only 1.05 — barely breakeven. The raw strategy needs ALL the filters to be profitable
  2. Avg loss (₹2,558) >> avg win (₹1,480) — losers are 1.7× bigger than winners. Need 60%+ win rate just to break even
  3. Monthly volatility is high — one bad month (May 2025) can wipe 3 good months
  4. Only 73 trades in 2 years — small sample for statistical confidence

Risk:Reward reality check

At 200 OTM with 100pt spread:

  • Collect ~₹18 premium
  • Risk ~₹82 (100pt spread - 18pt credit)
  • Risk:Reward = 4.5:1 (risk ₹4.50 to make ₹1)
  • At 79.5% win rate: EV = (0.795 × ₹18) - (0.205 × ₹82) = ₹14.3 - ₹16.8 = −₹2.5/trade

Wait — that's negative! The PF of 1.39 says it's profitable, but the R:R math shows negative EV. The discrepancy is because:

  • Not all losers hit max loss (many exit at expiry with partial credit)
  • The 50% profit booking changes the math (you exit winners early, but losers run to SL)

The edge is thin. It exists but it's not large. The filters are doing heavy lifting.

Recommended Rules for Paper Trading

Based on the analysis, here are the optimized rules:

Nifty Premium Mill — Optimized Rules


Entry:     Tuesday after 1:00 PM
VIX gate:  Only trade when VIX is 12-16
Direction: Above 20 SMA → Bull Put | Below → Bear Call
Sell:      200 points OTM
Buy:       300 points OTM (100pt spread)
Target:    Book 50% of credit collected
SL:        Exit if Nifty touches short strike
Time:      Let expire if deep OTM on expiry day

Expected:  ~80% win rate, ~2-3 trades/month, ~₹2,000/month/lot
Capital:   ₹50,000 per lot (spread margin)
