Overview

Paper trading the Nifty Premium Mill strategy using optimized rules from the 2-year backtest.

Optimized Rules (Backtested)

  • Entry: Tuesday after 1 PM
  • VIX gate: 12-16 only
  • Direction: Above 20 SMA → Bull Put | Below → Bear Call
  • Sell: 200 points OTM
  • Buy: 300 points OTM (100pt spread)
  • Target: Book 50% of credit collected
  • SL: Exit if Nifty touches short strike
  • Expiry: Let expire if deep OTM
