Technical Analysis (applying wiki frameworks)

1. Bollinger Band Position

  • Price has violently broken above the upper BB on the monthly chart
  • The breakout candle is a massive single green spike — far outside the band
  • This is a classic parabolic extension — price is stretched extremely far from the mean
  • BB mean reversion principle: the further from the band, the higher the reversion risk

2. Volume Mismatch Check (per Volume Mismatch.Md)

  • A parabolic SME spike with thin liquidity = high suspicion of operator-driven move
  • Upper circuits on SME stocks = very low liquidity = hard to exit at quoted price
  • ⚠️ This is exactly the "retail trap at top" pattern described in volume mismatch concept

3. RSI Reading

  • RSI(9) showing ~93 — extreme overbought territory
  • RSI MA(21) and MA(3) both elevated
  • On a monthly timeframe, RSI 90+ historically precedes sharp reversals
  • This is as overbought as it gets on monthly

4. Support & Resistance (per Support And Resistance.Md)

  • Stock rangebound ~20–50 for years (2018–2025)
  • Spike has no historical resistance above — pure price discovery
  • But equally no support structure below the spike — fall would be fast with no floor until 50–60 range

5. SME/Micro-cap Specific Risk

  • Operator-driven moves common in SME (previously flagged for GRAPHISAD-SM)

  • Classic SME pump signature — when circuits reverse, retail gets trapped
