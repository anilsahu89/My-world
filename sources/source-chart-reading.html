Summary (2 lines)

A rules cheat-sheet synthesizing MA, VWAP, AVWAP, OH/OL, Bollinger Bands, overbought/oversold, and Moh-Maya into one page of "what to actually do." Includes specific numeric thresholds (HM 86 monthly = overbought, HM 15 RSI flat in oversold, AVWAP 80% respect rate).

Key takeaways

  • OH/OL = resistance/support for the day; if the 10-min sustains the break, market runs the opposite direction.
  • Moving average rules:
  • Price follows average — both up = bullish, both down = bearish.
  • In uptrend, when the 20/50 SMA gap narrows and price bounces → best entry.
  • Price below 200 SMA = strong bearish.
  • Price repeatedly rejected at 20 SMA, then bounces off 200 SMA → usually breaks 20 SMA upward.
  • VWAP rules: intraday-only; above = buy, below = sell; don't trade VWAP alone — combine with 30-min/1-hr candles; enter near VWAP; not traded every day. Bullish sign: big 1st-hour candle, then small candles holding above VWAP.
  • AVWAP rules: use minimum 3 AVWAP lines; trade near AVWAP; intraday — when 10-min price rejects AVWAP, take the trade; for option sellers, wait for price to fully break AVWAP down then return to it before deciding. ~80% of the time price returns from AVWAP. For option buyers: if monthly OTP, price 100–200 wide, AVWAP flat and price sustains above all day → buy; high probability of doubling next day. On option charts, plot AVWAP from 4–5 months back (since birth).
  • Exit/SL: exit is more important than entry. 20 SMA on day TF = exit zone; usually broken only via gap up/down or a big candle (no chance to run). Static S/R also good exit. "20 SMA opposite side = best entry."
  • Trading rule: market near resistance → focus on top losers; near support → focus on top gainers.
  • Overbought/oversold:
  • Overbought = stock over-held by retailers; oversold = big money (FIIs/DIIs/punters) selling.
  • A crashed stock with too many retailers won't rise until retailers exit.
  • HM > 86 on monthly = overbought → expect small correction in 1–2 months; on day TF ~92+.
  • Trading decision: when weekly strength hits resistance AND daily gives a sell signal.
  • Easy to trade overbought, hard to trade oversold.
  • Oversold entry: when HM RSI hits 15 and goes flat, then starts to curve → take the trade.
  • Bollinger Band rules:
  • Uptrend + middle BB line rising → buy; bring to 20 SMA on smaller TF + buy signal → buy.
  • Market stuck in one place + BB narrow + BB blast → big move likely. But if BB was not narrow and it blasts → likely a trap.
  • Day-TF BB event → 7–28 days effect; hourly → 2–3 days effect.
  • Confirmation: BB blast + next day gap up + sustain = confirmed blast.
  • Moh-Maya: big news + day-TF doji-type candle outside the band → next day when 1-hr closes above VWAP → take the trade. Safe version: buy monthly expiry option of ₹60–100 near close; book 50% on double, 25% at 20 SMA, else hedge; SL below doji close.
  • Demand/Supply (RSI-based): use a TradingView RSI-based demand/supply indicator for auto zones.
