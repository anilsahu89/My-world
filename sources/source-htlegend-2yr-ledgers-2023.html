The swing data checklist (as disclosed)

1. OI build-ups, last 20 sessions: sum(long build-ups) − sum(short build-ups), counting only entries with positive volume change; ignore short covering / long unwinding. Net negative = downtrend.

2. Net OI change: PE OI change − CE OI change (negative = bearish), plus its day-over-day trend.

3. OI chain: call writing > put writing near ATM = bearish (closer to spot = stronger signal); reverse = bullish.

4. Price–PCR divergence: price down + PCR rising = bullish (enter on break of previous peak); price up + PCR falling = bearish. Shown as the repeated bottom-picker on Asian Paints.

5. OI percentile vs price percentile: price down + OI percentile up = bearish continuation; price up + OI percentile down = bullish continuation. Called "very accurate."

6. Max Pain divergence: Max Pain rising while price falls = rare = buy zone.

7. IVP regime switch: IVP > 80 → sell OTM (bearish → short call, bullish → short put); IVP < 20 → buy ITM. "Not always a seller — adjust to the market."

8. Entry sniping: price vs HTLB line (in-house) on M5/M15 + order-flow "delta bars" above/below a line for the exact entry.
