Summary (2 lines)

The early mentorship session that first codifies BB Trap rules and hedging methods. Adds the RSI-50 filter, the "midline breaker" BB usage for choppy markets, and detailed hedge math (ITM vs ATM vs OTM vs OTM hedging). More raw/teaching-style than the later BB Trap PDF.

Key takeaways

  • Price follows the higher time frame. Always follow 1-hour TF; use 30-min for entry.
  • RSI rule (hourly): never sell until RSI < 50 (the "blue, water" level); never buy until RSI > 50.
  • 20 SMA as SL/exit: stay in trade as long as 20 SMA isn't breached and 50 RSI doesn't cross. Above 20 SMA = buy zone; below = sell zone.
  • Midline breaker: when 20 SMA gets breached repeatedly (choppy), use Bollinger Bands instead — upper = resistance, lower = support. BB blast on hourly after consolidation = "big money" trade; naked option OK here.
  • BB Trap rules (formalized here):
  1. 15-min / 30-min / 1-hour TF.
  2. Good gap up/down open only.
  3. Min 1 candle closed outside the band.
  4. Next candle inside band AND closes below low of outside-band candle.
  5. Following candles stay below VWAP and sustain → then short.
  6. Pattern stays for the full day. Take ATM options.
  • Hedge methods for BB Trap:
  • ITM vs ATM: buy ITM (70%+ chips) + sell ATM. When premium is high; used intraday; expects smaller movement. Risk = buying − selling. Even if market stays flat → profitable.
  • OTM vs OTM: buy 1-strike OTM + sell further OTM. When chart reading says big move is coming. Max risk = small debit. Example (SBI): buy 370 PE @9.10 + sell 360 PE @6.70 → max risk ₹2.40 for the month; profit if SBI drops even to 380 from 398.
  • Future hedge: short future + short ATM put (high VIX) or buy call (low VIX).
  • Option hedge rule of thumb: if buying call, sell 2 strikes above; if buying put, sell 2 strikes below. Or: sell ATM when taking future.
  • Lower BB = support, upper BB = resistance — price may turn from either band.
