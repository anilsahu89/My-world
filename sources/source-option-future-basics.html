OPTION & FUTURE BASICS (NK@STOCKTALK Academy)

Ingested: 2026-06-23

Summary (2 lines)

The comprehensive foundations deck — defines the instruments (equity, futures, options), expiry mechanics, MTM settlement, strike-price classification, and the chips/hawa (intrinsic/time value) model that underpins every NK strategy. Also introduces VIX, hedging, and the basic indicator set (candlesticks, RSI, MA, VWAP, BB).

Key takeaways

  • Two instrument families: equity (underlying) vs derivatives (futures & options) — derivatives have no intrinsic value; they derive it from the underlying.
  • Future = right and obligation to buy/sell at a predetermined price on expiry. Index & stock futures expire on the last Thursday of the month (previous trading day if Thursday is a holiday).
  • Expiry types: near / next / far month. Near month has highest liquidity → trade it.
  • MTM (Mark to Market): daily settlement — closing vs opening price × lot size credited/debited each day. Must maintain margin + cover MTM debits or broker squares off.
  • Option = right without obligation. Call = buy (चढेगा), Put = sell (पडेगा). You pay a premium that decays to zero by expiry (like insurance).
  • Strike classification (relative to current price):
  • ATM — strike closest to current price (≈ no chips, mostly hawa)
  • ITM — has intrinsic value + time value; deeper ITM = more chips, less hawa
  • OTM — only time value (hawa); goes to zero on expiry
  • Chips = intrinsic value, Hawa = time value. Formula: Premium − Intrinsic Value = Time Value. "जो चिप्स है वो अपना है, जो हवा है वो सपना है" (chips are real, hawa is a dream).
  • Critical: option price is driven by the future price of the underlying, NOT the spot — except on expiry day after 12 noon.
  • VIX relationship: higher VIX → rising premium (good for buyers); lower VIX → falling premium (good for sellers). Never sell options when VIX is high.
  • Hedging = reducing risk via futures/options combos; reduces margin requirement dramatically. Three market participants: speculators (~4%), hedgers (~5%, the smart money), arbitrageurs (~92%, who lose to hedgers).
  • Hedging example: buy 1 lot 32000 CE + sell equal ATM CE monthly → works in uptrend + rangebound, protects against ~800-pt swing low.

Numeric rules / data captured

  • Lot sizes (Jan 2021): NIFTY 75, BANKNIFTY 25, FINNIFTY 40; stock futures vary (full list in source: ACC 500, RELIANCE 250, TCS 300, etc.)
  • Futures contract value ≥ ₹5 lakh at introduction
  • Margin ~20% of full notional for futures → 5× leverage (and 5× risk)
