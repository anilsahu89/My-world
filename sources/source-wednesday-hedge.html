Summary

Wednesday Bank Nifty option strategy exploiting 18-hour theta decay. Sell weekly OTM CE+PE on Wednesday, buy next week same strikes. Premium melts Thursday morning.

Execution Structure (Bank Nifty Spot 36700)

Entry Actions (Wednesday, preferably after 3:00 PM)

ActionStrikeSidePremium
SELL36900 (OTM)Weekly CE~₹175-200
SELL36500 (OTM)Weekly PE~₹175-200
BUY36900 (same strike)Next week CEMarket
BUY36500 (same strike)Next week PEMarket
  • Combined sell premium: ₹350-400

Why It Happens (4 Reasons)

  1. Low VIX Wednesday: 18-hour game — Thursday opening Call+Put premium reduces within 15-30 min
  2. Sell on Wednesday, don't buy (can buy next week if VIX high)
  3. From spot: sell same-week 200 up (Call) + 200 down (Put); buy next-week same strikes
  4. On expiry: sell-side premium reduces speedily; next-week tries to reach ATM → premium increases → book on opening

Key Rules

  • Use BASKET ORDER for low margin + mistake prevention
  • Book profit per your emotions and exit
  • Best after 3:00 PM Wednesday (even just before 3:30 works)
