Summary (2 lines)

The mechanics-disclosure episode the Aug-2026 ledger video withheld: a full daily-timeframe swing rulebook walked trade-by-trade on 20 years of Reliance — relative-strength pullback entries with end-of-day breakout confirmation, +3.6% price targets (~20% ROI via a ~5.5×-leveraged vega-positive long-option instrument), breakeven ratchet at +1.5%, and a loss-recovery "trade sequence." Also the money plan: invest 1/5 of capital in equities, pledge it, swing-trade on the collateral. Claims remain unaudited and the instrument itself is still gated.

The rulebook (as disclosed — unverified)

Filters (daily timeframe, F&O counters only):

1. HTLB Delta green — a 68-parameter data score; green = bullish data regime. Must stay green through the pullback (may turn red only after price is above the line).

2. HTLB1 rising during the price pullback — HTLB1 is Reliance's relative strength vs CNX 100 ("receiving more funds than the top 100"); stock dipping while RS rises = the top-100 average is dipping harder = accumulation. Rising RS into a falling price is the load-bearing signal.

3. HTLB1 above the dotted HTLB2 line — below it, "nothing to watch" (regime gate that skips whole declines).

4. Swing line blue (not black).

5. Proximity filter: price must pull back near the blue swing line — "far from the blue line" setups are skipped even when everything else aligns (enforced repeatedly; quality over frequency).

Entry:

  • Alert at the setup's peak (e.g. ₹1466.20); enter next day when the peak breaks, near the closing time — end-of-day confirmation, never the intraday first break.

Exits:

  • Target: +3.6–3.7% price move (Reliance-specific; "generally 3–3.5%") ≈ +20% ROI on margin (~5.5× effective leverage: 1% price ≈ 4.6% ROI).
  • Breakeven ratchet: at +1.5% price move, stop moves to entry. Breakeven trades marked dashed, not counted.
  • Loss (X): daily close below the reference line before +1.5% — "points given to the market," framed as "business investment," tracked in points.
  • Loss-recovery "trade sequence": the next trade's requirement becomes target + points previously given up — the winner must recover the prior loss. Repeats until the sequence closes.

The instrument (profile only — exact structure gated):

  • Vega-positive long-option structure, ~₹85k/lot. Claimed: profits on gap ups; "jackpot" on gap downs (IV doubles → ~40); ~4–5% ROI if price stays flat to expiry; "protection lines" below entry for small adverse moves.
  • Internal tension worth noting: a vega-positive structure that also earns while flat to expiry is not a plain long strangle — only a calendar-type spread behaves that way, and that carries its own gap risk. Undisclosed = unverifiable.

Money management:

  • ₹1 Cr capital → ₹20L (1/5) into equity investments → pledge → ~₹16L collateral margin → swing capital; "under-leveraged, not leverage."
  • Profit split: 50% to bank, 50% reinvested → re-pledged (compounding flywheel).
  • ~₹34L deployed per trade → ~₹6.9–10L per target hit. ~₹97L profit by trade 16 claimed on Reliance.
  • Frequency: ~80 trades/year across ~65 counters ≈ 6–7/month.

Claims vs evidence

  • 302% p.a. ROI, RR 1:1200, Max DD 0.57% (description/investment series) — escalating, unaudited, and the "800+ years of testing" is 200+ counters of overlapping history, not independent years. ₹1M "beat my system" challenge is marketing, not audit.
  • Reliance walkthrough result (~9 targets, several breakevens, 1 recovered loss in 10 trades) — self-scored on his own indicators, no ledger shown for these trades; breakevens excluded from the sheet flatters the sequence.
  • The loss-recovery sequence is the most dangerous idea in the video — escalating targets after losses is martingale-lite; sequence risk compounds exactly when the system is failing. psychology and Keshav's fixed-₹6k-risk rule both reject it.
  • Same eligibility/WhatsApp funnel as the ledger video.

What's genuinely interesting (for the thread)

  • The RS-vs-index pullback entry with EOD-confirmed breakout converges with NK's two-candle-close rule and outperformance doctrine — two unconnected systems demanding confirmation before breakout entry.
  • HTLB1 (RS vs CNX 100) is the same family as htl rotation — relative strength as the fund-flow proxy, applied per-stock instead of per-asset-class.
  • The breakeven ratchet at ~40% of target distance is a clean answer to our bb-blast backtest failure ("exit stack fights itself") — worth testing.
  • Pledge-margin funding (invest 1/5, swing on collateral) is a legitimate, commonly-used Indian structure — first source here to lay it out.
