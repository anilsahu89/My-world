Actually disclosed rules (usable, but unvalidated):

  • Equity booking: buy a stock, exit 50% of quantity at +100% (e.g. 32 → 64); the remaining 50% held "forever" — that's what grows unrealized P&L and keeps charges tiny.
  • Monday loss-closure: weekend chart review; if a position is below entry at Friday close, close Monday at market. Target bookings any day; loss bookings mostly Mondays.
  • Regime dial (gold vs Nifty fund flow): green = money rotating into gold → trade gold option writing; funds flowing back into Nifty → trade index. Claims the gold era ran ~Oct 2024–Apr 2026, and that Nifty's 24k→25k rise in early 2025 happened while funds flowed out (distribution). Entries taken near candle lows, not highs.

Philosophy worth keeping (echoes Psychology / Regime Mapping):

  • "Drawdowns and risk we control; profits the market decides." Low trade count / longer-hold positions = fewer executions = lower brokerage drag (their charges ≈ 0.1–0.5% of profits).
  • Consistency over magnitude: 10%/month compounding beats hunting 100% months. Diversify engines so "if one part is weaker, the second takes over" (swings in bull, expiry + gold in bear).
