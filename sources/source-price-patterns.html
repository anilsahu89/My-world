Price Patterns

Source

  • File: Price Patterns.pdf
  • Instructor: Nitish Kumar (NK StockTalk)

Summary

Comprehensive catalog of price patterns — both continuation and reversal types — with chart examples from Nifty, Bank Nifty, and individual stocks.

Two Categories

1. Continuation Patterns

After brief pause in ongoing trend, prior trend resumes. Formed on low volumes.

Pattern Description
Rectangle Price bounded by parallel S/R lines (trading range). Breakout in direction of prior trend.
Triangle Two converging trendlines. Breakout from 2/3rd length. Target = 1/2 base from breakout.
- Ascending Flat resistance, rising support → bullish
- Descending Flat support, falling resistance → bearish
- Symmetrical Both converging → breakout can be either way
Flag Two parallel trendlines (15-20 candles). Smaller timeframe = stronger impact.
Pennant Like symmetrical triangle but forms faster (15-20 candles). Temporary halt after good rally.

2. Reversal Patterns

Signal change in current trend. Larger timeframe = higher impact. Topping patterns shorter & more volatile; bottom formation takes longer.

Pattern Description
Head & Shoulders Three peaks: left shoulder, higher head, right shoulder. Neckline break = reversal. Target = head-to-neckline distance.
Inverse H&S Three troughs: left shoulder, lower head, right shoulder. Neckline break = bullish reversal.
Double Top (M) Two failed attempts at resistance → bearish reversal
Double Bottom (W) Two failed attempts at support → bullish reversal
Triple Top Three failed attempts at resistance → bearish reversal
Triple Bottom Three failed attempts at support → bullish reversal

Volume Confirmation

  • Breakout with higher volume = more impactful
  • Breakout can be accompanied by a price gap
  • Low volume during pattern formation (consolidation), high volume on breakout

Timeframe Impact

  • Higher timeframe → greater impact
  • Examples span 2-hour, 3-hour, daily, weekly charts
