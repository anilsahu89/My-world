21 CANDLESTICKS (Every trader should know)

Original file: ../21_Candlesticks.pdf (2.4 MB, ~1290 lines extracted — the longest source) Ingested: 2026-06-23

Summary (2 lines)

A comprehensive Western candlestick-pattern catalogue — single-candle (doji family, hammer, hanging man, marubozu, spinning top, high wave), two-candle (engulfing, harami, piercing, dark cloud, tweezers, kicking), and three/five-candle patterns (morning/evening star, three soldiers/crows, rising/falling three methods, abandoned baby, ladder bottom). This is a reference encyclopedia, not NK-specific strategy.

Key takeaways

Single-candle patterns: - Doji = equilibrium, indecision. Variants: long-legged (large upper shadow, weak if close < midpoint), gravestone (rallied then closed at open — ominous at tops), dragonfly (sold off then returned to open — often resolves bullish). - Hanging man (after uptrend) & Hammer (after downtrend) — lower shadow ≥ 2× body, tiny/no upper shadow; confirm next day. Color: black body stronger signal than white. - Marubozu = no/short shadows, large body → extreme conviction (white = buyers, black = sellers); watch next day for follow-through. - Spinning top = small body + small shadows + low volume = disinterest. - High wave = small body + very long both shadows = tug-of-war, indecision, possible trend shift.

Two-candle patterns: - Bullish/Bearish Engulfing — 2nd candle's real body fully engulfs the 1st (shadows need not be engulfed). - Harami (bullish/bearish) — small 2nd body inside large 1st body; reversal signal. - Piercing (bullish) / Dark Cloud Cover (bearish) — 2nd candle closes > halfway into prior body. - Tweezers (top/bottom) — two sessions share identical/near-identical highs or lows. - Kicker — explosive: gap open above prior high+close (bullish) or below prior low+close (bearish); "shock event."

Three/five-candle patterns: - Morning Star / Evening Star — long body → small star (gap) → long body reversing ≥50% into day-1 body. - Three White Soldiers (bullish, post-decline+consolidation) / Three Black Crows (bearish, each close lower). - Rising/Falling Three Methods — continuation: long body + 3 small opposite bodies contained within it + long body resuming. - Abandoned Baby (rare, no confirmation needed) — long black → doji gap-down → long white gap-up. - Ladder Bottom, Three Stars in the South, Concealing Baby Swallow, Upside Gap Two Rabbits — rarer reversals. - On Neck / In Neck / Thrusting — downtrend continuation (each closes slightly higher into prior black body). - Side-by-Side White Lines, Homing Pigeon, Matching Low, Deliberation, Advance Block, Mat Hold, J-Hook, Cradle, Scoop, Stick Sandwich, Dumpling Top, Fry Pan Bottom, Tower Top/Bottom, Three Line Strike, Meeting Line, Separating Line, Thrusting Line, 8-10-12-13 new price lines — all catalogued with recognition criteria.

How to use this in the wiki

This source is a pattern dictionary. When other sources mention a candlestick by name (e.g. Moh-Maya doji, BB trap "spinning top"), link back here rather than re-explaining. Individual patterns don't need their own concept pages unless they recur across multiple strategies.
