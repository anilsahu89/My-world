MOVING AVERAGE (NK, handwritten-style notes)

MOVING AVERAGE (NK, handwritten-style notes)

Original file: ../moving average.pdf (388 KB, ~42 lines extracted) Ingested: 2026-06-23

Summary (2 lines)

Compact Hindi notes tying moving-average theory to VWAP, OH/OL, and the MOH-MAYA pattern (NK's positional reversal candle). Defines the dynamic vs static S/R model and the rule "trade only when price meets its average."

Key takeaways

  • Average = the "soul" of price (price is the body). Two S/R types: dynamic (moving averages) and static (horizontal levels); a third "future" S/R comes with experience.
  • Universal rule: if a gap opens between price and its average, either price returns to average or average catches up — trade decisions only at that meeting point. "हर जगह jump नहीं लगाना है।"
  • 20 SMA tactic: falling price touching 20 SMA → short; price closing above 20 SMA → long.
  • Bullish when price > both 20 DMA and 50 DMA; bearish when below both. Best entry = price comes to both averages and curves upward.
  • Always follow the bigger time frame. Monthly = best for investing.
  • VWAP is the one intraday-only average. Price above VWAP → don't sell; below → only sell. But "VWAP की hunting होती है" — don't trade on VWAP alone; enter only when VWAP curves up/down, near VWAP, after the 1st hour candle. Use 1-hr chart, 10-min for entry. Initiate intraday trades only after 3 hours (≈12:15), near VWAP.
  • OH/OL: when formed, acts as resistance/support for the day; if broken, expect strong move that day.
  • Not every day is tradeable — trade only 6–8 times a month.
  • Range-bound day signal: 1-hr candle with huge upper+lower wicks → range bound until its low/high breaks.
  • Entry tactic: if 1-hr candle has wicks both ways but next 2 candles are small and above VWAP → wait till 12:15, buy near VWAP.
  • MOH-MAYA pattern (positional reversal): on day TF, one candle closes outside the Bollinger Band; next day's close must be below previous day's close (bullish case) / above (bearish case) and back inside the band. Claimed to have power to cover all past losses. Action: take positional trade without fail — build a future hedge, or buy call / sell put since trend is known.

⚠️ Note on the MOH-MAYA claim

"Cover all past losses" is an aspirational claim from the source, not validated data. Treat as a high-conviction setup the author likes, not a proven edge.
