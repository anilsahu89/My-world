OH-OL Chart Reading — Open High / Open Low Option Selling

OH-OL Chart Reading — Open High / Open Low Option Selling

Source

  • File: OPEN HIGH OPEN LOW CHART READING.pdf
  • Instructor: Nitish Kumar (NK StockTalk)

Summary

Core strategy of OH-OL (Open High / Open Low) option selling — identifying stocks that hit their day's high or low in the first candle, then selling deep ITM options against them.

Key Teachings

What is OH-OL?

  • Open = High (OH): Stock hits its day's high in the first candle itself → unlikely to go higher → sell CE
  • Open = Low (OL): Stock hits its day's low in the first candle → unlikely to go lower → sell PE
  • These are high-probability intraday setups for option sellers

Core Philosophy

  • Treat deep ITM options as futures (chips = intrinsic value)
  • Sell the hawa (time value) component
  • Keep capital preserved for adjustments

Trading Process

  1. Identify OH/OL stocks from the day's first candle
  2. Check VWAP direction for bias
  3. Select deep ITM strike (high chips, minimal hawa)
  4. Sell the option → collect premium → wait for decay
  5. Exit when premium reduces sufficiently (typically by EOD)

Hedging with OH-OL

  • If trade goes against: use hedging techniques (covered positions, spreads)
  • Maintain capital reserve (60%) for adjustments
  • Don't over-leverage on single trade

Selection Criteria

  • Look for stocks with good volume in first candle
  • VWAP should confirm the OH/OL direction
  • Avoid stocks with wide daily range on low volume
