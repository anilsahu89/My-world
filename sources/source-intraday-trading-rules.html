Source: Intraday Trading Rules

Summary

Rule checklist for trend-aligned intraday trading using index direction, top gainers/losers, HM, Bollinger Bands, 9 SMA high, and VWAP.

Key Takeaways

  • Do not trade first 2 hours; best is to wait 3 hours and trade after 12:15 PM.
  • Check Nifty, Bank Nifty, and Sensex direction; skip if direction is unclear.
  • Rangebound definition: Nifty within about +/-50, Sensex/Bank Nifty within about +/-100.
  • In uptrend, select top gainers from Nifty 50/100/200/Midcap.
  • In downtrend, select top losers from those groups.
  • Plot HM, BB, 9 SMA high, and VWAP.
  • Main observation timeframe: 30 minutes.

Buy Rules

  • If BB is narrow, wait for BB blast.
  • If price breaks VWAP, comes near 20 SMA, bounces, and closes above VWAP, check HM.
  • HM buy and RSI above 60 required.
  • Entry near 20 SMA or just above VWAP.
  • SL: two 10-minute closes below 20 SMA; day low can be system SL if nearby.
  • Ride trade while price stays above 9 SMA high on 10-minute chart.
  • Exit on two closes below 9 SMA high, or one close below both 9 SMA high and 20 SMA.

Sell Rules

  • Mirror rules for downtrend.
  • HM sell and RSI below 45 required.
  • Entry near 20 SMA or just below VWAP.
  • SL: two 10-minute closes above 20 SMA; day high can be system SL if nearby.
