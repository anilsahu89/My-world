MA CONVERGENCE — Stock Market Basics (NK@STOCKTALK Academy)

Original file: ../STOCK MARKET BASICS CHART READING-CONVERGENCE OF MOVING AVERAGES-1.pdf (5.8 MB, ~500 lines extracted) Ingested: 2026-06-23

Summary (2 lines)

The master chart-reading framework: introduces the 20/50/100/150/200 EMA stack with color coding, the COMA (Correct Order of Moving Averages) trend-identification rule, convergence-based entries confirmed by Hilega-Milega, plus a full Bollinger Band playbook (squeeze/blast, trap, walk) and AVWAP setup. This is the spine NK's intraday method hangs on.

Key takeaways

  • Time-frame rule: larger TF = stronger trend. Periods to study: intraday 3–15 min, positional 1-hr, long-term daily/weekly.
  • EMA periods & color coding (NK house standard):
  • 20 EMA = BROWN
  • 50 EMA = GREEN
  • 100 EMA = BLUE
  • 150 EMA = PINK
  • 200 EMA = YELLOW
  • SMA formula: (A1+A2+…+An)/n. EMA weights recent prices more → more responsive.
  • Data-window rule: a 20-MA on a 5-min chart = last 20 5-min candles; on daily = last 20 days.
  • COMA (Correct Order of Moving Averages):
  • Uptrend: 20 (brown) on top → 50 → 100 → 150 → 200 (yellow) at bottom, all in correct order.
  • Downtrend: order reversed (yellow on top, brown at bottom).
  • Buy rule (uptrend convergence): 1. Check for convergence (all EMAs meet at a point) 2. Confirm COMA correct order 3. When price converges and moves up → quick up-move expected 4. Hilega-Milega must indicate BUY 5. SL = EMA where price is taking support 6. Exit only when price closes below EMA support
  • Sell rule (downtrend): mirror — convergence + reverse COMA + HM sell signal.
  • VWAP = "the station" — enter the trade near VWAP only; never chase price away from VWAP. Around 1:30 PM price tends to return to 20-MA/VWAP.
  • Two correction types: time-wise (price waits for average to catch up) and price-wise (price drops back to average).
  • Price averaging: faster the price moves, sooner it returns to average (train analogy).
  • Bollinger Band playbook:
  • BB = volatility indicator; default 20-period SMA ± 2 standard deviations.
  • Squeeze → blast: bands narrow 10–15 min then blast both sides → moves to upper or lower limit; buy if candle rides upper band, short if rides lower; stay till it leaves the band.
  • BB Walk = high-probability trend continuation when price walks along a band.
  • Trap pattern: candle closes outside upper band → expect fall; watch 3rd candle — if it closes above VWAP, buy.
  • Intraday rules (15-min): buy only above 20 SMA (black line); sell below it; confirm with VWAP + HM.
  • Jackpot rule: market gaps + next candle closes outside upper band without touching the band → price falls, take short. If it recovers above VWAP → don't short (trap for retailers).
  • NO-trade zone: if candle doesn't close below previous candle's low → don't short.
  • Time-frame sequence for intraday: daily → hourly → 15-min (all must align below/above 20 SMA / BB mid for a clean short/long).
  • SL: just below VWAP + ATR (add 15–20% of ATR below VWAP to absorb volatility). ATR = volatility indicator.
  • Support/Resistance: floor/ceiling analogy; previous support = next support, previous resistance = next resistance; don't take long until price closes above last resistance. Roles flip on breakout.
  • AVWAP setup: select 2 anchor dates (e.g. Nifty 7500 low on 23 Mar, 2nd low 18 May 2020) — FIIs/DIIs enter at anchor rates → must buy when candle touches anchored rate + HM buy confirmation. Daily TF for short-term, 5/15-min for intraday, weekly for long-term.
  • Option selling near AVWAP: sell PE in uptrend / CE in downtrend, 1-hr chart, only when price touches the anchor; sell option opposite to the trend.
  • Rangebound vs trading detection: BB narrow = rangebound; Fractal indicator works in rangebound — sell option inside the box, don't sell outside, exit if fractal breaks.
  • Two NK patterns previewed here: Three-Step Pattern (price closes above prior highs slowly, 1-hr, 70–100 pts) and Killer Wave (candles close lower one after another, short immediately, 1-hr, 70–100 pts).
