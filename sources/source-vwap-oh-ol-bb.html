VWAP with OH-OL, BB & BB Trap — Combined Intraday Rules

VWAP with OH-OL, BB & BB Trap — Combined Intraday Rules

Source

  • File: VWAP WITH OH,OL, BB, BB TRAP-converted-compressed.pdf
  • Instructor: Nitish Kumar (NK StockTalk)

Summary

Comprehensive intraday chart reading rules combining VWAP, OH-OL identification, Bollinger Bands, and BB Trap/BB Blast scenarios.

Intraday Setup Rules

  1. Always check: (a) Day Chart, (b) Yesterday's Low, (c) Today's Open
  2. No predefined market view — let price action decide
  3. VWAP is most effective for intraday (incorporates volume)
  4. Combine VWAP with Previous S/R and Bollinger Bands
  5. Inexperienced traders → use 1-hour chart
  6. Trade only when full confirmation exists — not every day

VWAP Rules (Recap)

  • VWAP up + price up → every dip is a buy
  • VWAP down + price down → every rise is a sell
  • After 12:45 PM, price returns to VWAP if move was missed

VWAP + BB Combined Rules

  • BB upper/lower bands = major S/R
  • Price opens at BB → wait for proper Band Blast
  • Price above VWAP + above Upper Band → wait for Band Blast
  • Price fails below VWAP → next target = 20 SMA
  • 20 SMA break requires big candle, fast manner
  • If price backs from 20 SMA → don't buy until close above day's highest close

BB Blast Failure Rules

  1. If Band Blast fails → think sell trade only
  2. If price starts sustaining → wait for 20 SMA break or day's high break
  3. If 20 SMA broken → short sell with SL at day's high

BB Trap Failure Rules

  1. BB Trap formed by long inverted candle → if price can't cut the high → SL hunting
  2. If you missed the candle → notice price testing previous candle's high → exit
  3. Price leaves band and trades at same level → exit (SL hunting coming)
  4. 5-candle rule: 5+ candles at same level → market reverses
  5. If HM red & green lines meet → exit
  6. Buy when high of the day is broken

BB Trap + VWAP Reversal

  1. After BB Trap, if price comes back & sustains above VWAP → should have cut VWAP in one go
  2. If one candle cuts 20 SMA → reverse the trade
  3. 20 SMA is the 24-hour average — if broken, price was above it 24 hrs → will fall
  4. SL = high of the candle that broke 20 SMA
  5. HM being blue confirms downward momentum

HM Signal on BB Trap

  • If Volume, Price & Strength all at same place → price won't go up
  • If strength reducing (e.g., 79 → 68) and FLAT → bearish signal
