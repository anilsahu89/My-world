Three Types of Risk

  1. Known Risk — Trader knows profit start point and risk amount
  2. Unknown Risk — Trade taken without study (tips, TV news)
  3. Planned Risk — Risk planned before trade; minimize/break-even the loss

Are all hedgers profitable? No. Mistakes happen in entry area and adjustment area. Market is dynamic → must plan risk and master adjustment.

Hedge 23 — Core Concept

  • Uses ITM options (70%+ chips)
  • Risk area is very small
  • Room for proper adjustment
  • Exits with less/no profit even after incorrect execution
  • Target = hawa of the deep ITM option

Execution Timing

  • VIX high: high premium, high profit
  • VIX normal: normal premium, less profit
  • Difficult to get monthly deep ITM without hawa — usually available only when VIX > 30 or < 13
  • Weekly ITM best on Wednesday after 2:30 PM or expiry day

Design Steps

  1. Mark S/R on weekly chart with BB (upper = resistance, lower = support)
  2. Mark adjustment area
  3. Check trend using line chart on weekly TF (above 20SMA = very bullish)
  4. Pick next month ITM strike near support (daily TF)

Bullish Example (April 3, 2022)

  • BN Spot = 37148 | BN May FUT = 37365
  • Buy BN May FUT @ 37365
  • Short BN May 35000 CE @ 2890
  • Hawa target = 525 points (35000 + 2890 − 37365)
  • Margin ≈ ₹1.7 lakh

Adjustment When Wrong

  • Sell current month CE at resistance strike
  • If resistance = 37400:
  • Sell 37000 CE @ 1060, SL @ 38060
  • Or sell 37500 CE @ 800, SL @ 38300
  • Or sell 38000 CE @ 580, SL @ 38580

Bearish Example

  • Short BN May FUT @ 37365
  • Short BN May 38500 PE @ 1990
  • Chips = 1135, Hawa = 855

Stock Example (SBIN)

  • Spot = 508 | FUT = 511
  • Buy SBIN May FUT @ 511
  • Short SBIN May 480 CE @ 44
  • Hawa target = 13 points (480 + 44 − 511)

Hedge with Options (Not Future)

  • Buy APR 7th 36900 @ 260 (almost no hawa)
  • Short BN May 35000 CE @ 2890
  • 3 advantages: (i) no MTM, (ii) no adjustment when market crashes, (iii) less margin

Key Notes

  • CE side adjustment is easier than PE side
  • Only enemy = VIX (sudden rise adds premium to short position)
  • When ITM option value < spot/FUT → market is bullish (indicator for naked buy)
  • Start positional first → then intraday; Index first → then stocks

Flat Wave Pattern (Cash Trade)

  • Stock falls to ATL, then rises above 20 & 50 SMA weekly
  • Big move (200-1000%+), then consolidates in range
  • Price comes to 50SMA, then moves up sharply
  • Wait 5, 10, 12 weeks → price breaks range top
  • Weekly close above → 2-6 month positional trade → 40-200% profit
  • SL = 50SMA weekly close
