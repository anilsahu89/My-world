Source: A-19 Double, A100 Expiry, Time Track

Source: A-19 Double, A100 Expiry, Time Track

Summary

Primary A-19 source. It describes a ratio-style options hedge where one option is bought and two options are sold, plus A100 monthly-expiry futures gap trading and time-track option selling.

A-19 Double

  • Structure: buy 1 lot, sell 2 lots.
  • Avoid when VIX is above 23 because large 200-300 point gaps can move outside the expected range.
  • Also avoid when VIX is falling from a convergence point, because a steady 500-600 point rally can occur.
  • Nifty example: buy monthly 16000 CE and sell two monthly 16300 CE when spot is near 15821.
  • Strike logic: buy strike plus buy premium gives the approximate sell strike, rounded to the nearest strike.
  • Keep buying premium slightly higher than total selling premium; the debit is the defined downside risk.
  • Weekly far OTM buy can reduce margin and protect against random overnight moves.
  • Can be built on CE or PE side, and in stocks, Nifty, Bank Nifty, gold, silver, or currency.

Claimed Payoff Behavior

  • Downside loss is limited to the buy-sell debit.
  • Rangebound market should be favorable due to sold premium decay.
  • Upside is protected up to a defined range, but loss begins if expiry moves beyond the upper breakeven.
  • Extra intraday income can be generated by selling far OTM options only when technical direction supports it.

A100 Expiry Arbitrage

  • Trades the gap between current-month and next-month futures on monthly expiry day.
  • If current future has premium over spot and next future has larger premium, sell current future and buy next future.
  • If current future is below spot, buy current future and short next future.
  • Entry windows: around 9:30 AM and again near 12:50 PM if the gap recreates.
  • Use basket orders.

Time Track Selling

  • Execute around 9:30 AM or 2:30 PM.
  • Short straddle or strangle on Nifty/Bank Nifty.
  • Sum-wise SL: total premium received plus about 10 points; exit if sustained for more than 2 minutes.
  • In stock options after the 15th of the month, short both call and put around 2:30 PM for small daily decay; source mentions Rs 1 SL on a Rs 7 premium example.

Backtest Notes

  • A-19 needs explicit payoff modeling by strike, premium, expiry, VIX, and adjustment rules.
  • A100 needs futures EOD/intraday data across expiry days.
  • Time Track is a separate intraday options-selling strategy; do not mix results with A-19.
