AVWAP — Introduction and Application

AVWAP — Introduction and Application

Source

  • File: AVWAP Intro and Application.pdf
  • Instructor: Nitish Kumar (NK StockTalk)

Summary

Foundational introduction to Anchored VWAP (AVWAP) — what it is, how it differs from regular VWAP, and its application in trading, investing, and options.

Key Teachings

What is AVWAP?

  • Anchored VWAP ties VWAP calculation to a specific price bar chosen by the trader
  • Same formula as regular VWAP, but you choose the starting point instead of it resetting daily
  • Starting point typically marks a shift in market psychology (significant high/low, earnings, news)

AVWAP vs Regular VWAP

FeatureRegular VWAPAnchored VWAP
Start pointFirst bar of dayTrader-chosen bar
End pointLast bar of dayMost recent bar
TimeframeIntraday onlyAny timeframe
ResetDailyNever (until re-anchored)

Cardinal Rules of AVWAP

  1. Anchor date = event date minus 1 day (always one day prior)
  2. For Indexes, use Futures (spot has no volume)
  3. For investment analysis, use Monthly chart; daily is valid for intraday only

Identifying Market Trend with AVWAP

  • Use 1-hour chart + 20 SMA — above 20 = uptrend, below = downtrend
  • Use 30-min chart — zigzag formation with downward trend = bearish (valid 10-15 days max)
  • On weekly/monthly chart showing zigzag downtrend → not worth buying/investing

Golden Investment Rule

  • On monthly chart, when price touches AVWAP without violating it → golden opportunity to invest
  • Invest in sector leaders with maximum index weightage — they recover fastest

Options Trading with AVWAP

  • Options above AVWAP → bullish → buy when price comes near AVWAP, SL below AVWAP
  • Options below AVWAP → bearish → sell when price comes near AVWAP, SL at/above AVWAP
  • Check underlying chart (Index Future / Stock) on 1H + 20 SMA before option trade
  • Always use current month options; after 20th shift to next month

Capital Rules

  • Naked option buy: max 10% of capital per trade
  • Naked option sell: max 40% of capital; keep 60% for adjustments

Market Correction Types

  1. Time-wise correction: Price moves sideways, forms averages — AVWAP adjusts on chart
  2. Price-wise correction: Market falls sharply with volume in short time
