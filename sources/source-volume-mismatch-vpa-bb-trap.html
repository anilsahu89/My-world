NK combines Volume Price Analysis (VPA) — the effort-vs-result principle from Anna Coulling/Wyckoff — with his existing BB Trap setup. The result is a volume-confirmed BB Trap that filters out false traps and identifies high-probability reversals. This is the bridge between NK's BB system and the VPA framework.

Core Concept: Volume Mismatch

Definition

A volume mismatch occurs when:

  • High volume appears on a candle, BUT
  • The price spread (range) is small — price barely moved despite the effort

This is Wyckoff's Effort vs Result law applied to Bollinger Bands:

> Volume = Effort. Price spread = Result. High effort + small result = the move is weak → reversal likely.

The Logic

  • If big volume pushed price outside the BB but price didn't sustain → smart money is distributing (selling into the breakout)
  • The breakout is a trap — retailers bought the breakout, smart money sold into them
  • Volume confirms the trap: the volume was real (effort happened), but price couldn't follow through (result was weak)

The Setup: Volume-Mismatch BB Trap

Step 1: BB Trap Formation

  1. Price moves outside the upper Bollinger Band (or lower band for inverse)
  2. This is the standard BB Trap trigger from Bb Trap

Step 2: Volume Check (the VPA filter)

  • Check the volume on the candle that went outside the band
  • High volume + candle closes back inside the band = volume mismatch
  • The high volume means effort was made, but price couldn't hold outside = result was weak

Step 3: Confirmation

  • Next candle should trade below VWAP on hourly timeframe
  • Or: closes below the previous candle's low
  • HM (Hilega Milega) should confirm bearish momentum (red above green, both falling)

Step 4: Entry

  • Enter on the confirmation candle
  • SL = high of the candle that went outside the BB (day's high)
  • Target = 20 SMA (the BB center line) or lower band

> ⚠️ Key filter: A BB Trap WITHOUT volume mismatch is NOT a valid trap signal in this framework. Volume is the confirmation that separates real traps from noise.

Recommendation for BB-v2

Add a volume mismatch filter to the scanner:

  1. On breakout candle: check if volume > 1.5× average volume
  2. If yes AND candle closes back inside BB within 2 candles → flag as trap, skip entry
  3. If yes AND candle sustains outside → valid breakout, enter
  4. If no (low volume) → flag as weak breakout, lower confidence

This would have filtered out the false breakouts that the BB-v2 scanner currently can't distinguish from real ones.

Worked Example (from video)

Setup

  1. Stock trading in a range with BB squeeze (narrow bands)
  2. Price breaks above upper BB with a large candle
  3. Volume is very high — looks like a strong breakout
  4. BUT: the candle closes back inside the upper band (long upper wick or red close)
  5. This is the volume mismatch — high effort, weak result

Action

  • Don't buy the breakout (retailers will)
  • Wait for next candle to confirm: trades below VWAP on hourly
  • Short sell with SL at the day's high (the wick that went outside)
  • Target: 20 SMA (BB center) or lower band

Result

  • Price falls back to 20 SMA — BB Trap confirmed
  • The high volume was smart money distributing into the breakout

Key Rules Summary

  1. Volume is the truth-teller — price lies, volume doesn't
  2. High volume + price failure outside BB = trap (this is the core signal)
  3. No volume = no trap — skip if volume is low on the breakout
  4. Always check VWAP after the trap candle — price must trade below VWAP for bearish trap
  5. SL = day high (the candle that went outside the band)
  6. Target = 20 SMA (BB center line) — price attracts to it
  7. Volume mismatch works on both sides — upper band (bearish trap) and lower band (bullish trap/Moh Maya)
  8. Combine with HM — HM red above green + falling = confirms bearish momentum
