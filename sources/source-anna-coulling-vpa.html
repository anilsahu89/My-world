Summary

Foundational text on Volume Price Analysis (VPA) — reading price action and volume together to "think like market insiders." Volume validates price movements and reveals accumulation, distribution, and market manipulation that price alone hides.

Three Core Pillars

1. Price + Volume = Insider View

  • Combine price action with volume to understand what market insiders (smart money) are doing
  • Volume validates price moves — price lies, volume doesn't
  • Reveals accumulation (smart money buying quietly) and distribution (smart money selling quietly)
  • Volume exposes market manipulation that pure price charts hide

2. Wyckoff's Three Laws

Coulling's VPA is built on Richard Wyckoff's framework:

Law Meaning
Supply & Demand Price moves based on imbalance between buyers (demand) and sellers (supply). Volume reveals which side is dominant.
Cause & Effect Accumulation/distribution phases (the "cause") build energy that determines the size of the subsequent markup/markdown (the "effect"). Longer consolidation = bigger move.
Effort vs Result Volume = effort; price spread = result. If high volume (effort) produces small price move (result), the move is weak — reversal likely. If low volume produces big move, move is strong.

3. Key Techniques

  • Volume at Price (VAP): Volume distribution across price levels (not time) — reveals where real S/R sits
  • Volume Price Analysis (VPA): Reading each candle's volume relative to its price spread
  • Buying/Selling Climaxes: High-volume exhaustion moves that mark trend ends
  • Anomaly detection: Volume spikes that don't match price action = warning signal

Key Vocabulary

Term Definition
Accumulation Smart money quietly buying during consolidation (the "cause" before markup)
Distribution Smart money quietly selling during consolidation (the "cause" before markdown)
Buying climax High-volume surge at the top — last retail buyers in, smart money exiting
Selling climax High-volume capitulation at the bottom — last retail sellers out, smart money entering
Narrow spread candle Small body — often signals congestion or uncertainty; meaningful when paired with high volume
Wide spread candle Large body — strong directional conviction; check volume to confirm
Effort vs Result Volume (effort) should produce proportional price move (result); divergence = trap
