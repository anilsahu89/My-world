OPTION CALCULATION — OP/FUT/HEDGE/ARBITRAGE (Mentorship Q&A, 6 Mar 2021)

Original file: ../OPTION CALCULATION OP FUT HEDGE ARBITRAGE.pdf (1 MB, ~290 lines extracted) Ingested: 2026-06-23

Summary (2 lines)

The most strategy-dense foundations source: turns chips/hawa into concrete trade designs — risk-defined option spreads, the "18-hour game" (expiry theta), rangebound (monthly+weekly same-strike) and volatile-market (4-leg) playbooks, plus future-hedging mechanics with worked margin/risk math.

Key takeaways

  • Weekly vs monthly options behave differently — never buy naked weekly options as positional (the price you pay is mostly hawa that dissolves).
  • OTM breakeven reality check: BN future 35380, weekly 35800 CE @391.50 → you only profit above 35800 + 391.50 = 36191.50. Below that, you lose. "If you don't know how to calculate chips and hawa, don't trade options."
  • Deeper ITM = cheaper effective cost even though absolute premium is higher (e.g. 34500 strike has only ₹225 hawa vs rest being real chips).
  • VIX ↔ options: rising VIX → premium added to OTM rapidly → only buy in rising VIX. Falling VIX → OTM loses premium rapidly → only sell.
  • Risk definition by trade type:
  • Positional Bank Nifty → buy ITM, sell ATM
  • Intraday → buy OTM, sell OTM
  • Bollinger Band Blast → buy OTM, sell further OTM
  • 18-hour game: sell a weekly OTM option at 3 PM Wed → on Thursday it loses (almost) all premium regardless of VIX, *as long as it doesn't become ATM. Daily theta ≈ premium/5 days.
  • Max profit calc (spread): (Short strike + premium received) − (Long strike + premium paid).
  • Rangebound play: buy Monthly + sell Weekly at the same strike → weekly dissolves faster than monthly; works best when VIX ~16–17 and range ≤500 pts.
  • Volatile market play: 4 legs (2 call + 2 put); sell an extra lot on the losing side to cover, square off extras intraday, carry only the 4 core overnight. If trend reverses → exit all, it's a trap.
  • 9 SMA on VIX (1-hr chart): once VIX closes above 9 SMA it shoots up, below it falls.
  • Option-sell zone via AVWAP: sell OTM put in uptrend / OTM call in downtrend near AVWAP resistance on hourly chart.
  • Future hedge mechanics (worked example, Bharti Airtel): buy Future + buy ITM-ish PE (570 PE: chips 34, hawa 8) + sell call above the PE strike (580 CE @7.10) → max risk drops to ~₹1. Adjustment: roll down to lower strikes (530 PE / 550 CE) if price falls; sell an extra call for extra profit in downtrend.
  • Pro tip: when your long option reaches 95% price + 5% chips, book it and adjust (also when liquidity starts drying up).

Numeric rules captured

  • Daily theta on weekly OTM ≈ premium / 5 per day
  • Margin per hedged lot ≈ ₹1.15 lakh (incl. extra sold lot)
  • Rangebound sweet spot: VIX 16–17, range ≤500 pts
