Summary (2 lines)

The definitive BB Trap manual: identification rules, 4 hedged trade variants (cash intraday, future+hedge, call spread, put spread), worked examples on Indigo/BankNifty, AVWAP option-selling rules, and the "Jackpot Trade" variant when all three timeframes align. This is the most complete BB Trap source.

Key takeaways

  • BB Trap = retailers trapped on a fake gap move. Price gaps up/down → retailers pile in → smart money sells into them → price collapses back below VWAP. SL: above high of the outside-band candle.
  • 4 ways to trade (choose by capital, risk, emotion):
  1. Cash intraday — sell short, MIS only, never NRML/carry.
  2. Short Future + sell ATM Put — directional + premium income. (Example: Indigo future 1672, short 1600 PE @67.95 → net +31 pts.)
  3. Sell Call above VWAP — naked or hedged (buy call 50 pts above sold strike). Hedged profit smaller (~8–9 pts) but no system SL needed.
  4. Buy 1-strike-away OTM Put + sell far OTM Put — defined risk = buy−sell premium. Example: buy 1660 PE @67 + sell 1620 PE @48 → max risk ₹19 for full month.
  • Jackpot Trade: BB Trap on all three TFs (15/30/60 min) simultaneously → very big move, occurs once in 2–3 months. "Very little SL hunting."
  • Frequency: 5–6 times/month in indices, 2–3 in a stock. Works in intraday only (positional uses daily TF).
  • Buy Monthly option to protect against premium loss in intraday.
  • When NOT to trade: lower-band BB Trap + Open=High, or upper-band BB Trap + Open=Low → avoid.
  • BB Trap + Three-Step pattern is supreme — ignore Hilega-Milega signals when both form together. Retailer trapping doesn't show on indicators until later.

AVWAP section (also in this PDF)

  • Anchors = where FIIs/DIIs enter. Find 2 fear-point lows on Nifty daily chart → set as anchors.
  • Option selling with AVWAP: enter near anchor, SL = anchor itself. If price closes above anchor → SL hit; below → stay in trade.
  • Rule: above anchor → buy option; below anchor → sell option. If both CE and PE under anchor → both go to 0.
  • Works across all instruments: stocks, futures, options, commodities, currencies.
  • Option chart: plot AVWAP from 4–5 months before the option's birth.
