Summary

NK's dedicated framework for using India VIX as an intraday timing trigger for option buying. Targeted specifically at option buyers who struggle with exits and momentum. Three pillars: VIX day-high break on 5-min chart, 30-minute holding rule, and strike selection by premium range.

Core Thesis

"Trading mein successful hone ka sirf ek tarika hai — exit aapko khud se aani chahiye." (The only way to succeed in trading is to learn to exit on your own.)

"Loss wo nahi hai jo aapne book kiya. Loss wo hai jab aap sahi the aur poora ride nahi kiya." (Loss is not what you booked — loss is when you were right but didn't ride it fully.)

Rule 1: Gap Up/Down + VIX Direction (Opening Setup)

Scenario VIX Condition Meaning Action
Gap Up India VIX down >4% Gap up will sustain Buy after 5-min close, SL = day low
Gap Down India VIX up >4% Gap down will sustain Sell after 5-min close, SL = day high

Critical details

  • Wait for 5-minute candle close before deciding
  • SL is closing-basis, NOT touch-basis — price must close above/below the level
  • The 4% VIX threshold is the filter — below 4%, the signal is weaker
  • If gap up + VIX down <4% → gap may not sustain → don't trade
  • Once gap sustains for 5 min → price runs into range-bound mode after that

Rule 2: VIX Day-High Break = Wave Strength Trigger

The Setup

  1. Open 5-minute chart of India VIX alongside the option chain
  2. Mark the VIX day-high level
  3. Watch for VIX to break (close above) its day-high on the 5-min candle

Interpretation

When VIX breaks day-high in... Wave Strength Expected Move
Single 5-min candle Strong — sustainable move Full ride (300→1300 type)
10 minutes Weaker 300→600/700, may not sustain
15 minutes Weaker Even less sustained
1-hour timeframe Very strong Effect lasts 2-3 days

If VIX does NOT break day-high on the 5-min chart → the wave is weak → don't expect the big move. It can reverse anytime.

VIX Direction Logic

  • India VIX only checked for PUT buying (put side)
  • For CALL buying: don't check VIX, check price + Hilega Milega
  • Rising VIX = price support — if VIX rises, the put side is supported, price won't easily fall
  • VIX and option premium should move simultaneously on the same 5-min candle

Rule 3: 30-Minute Holding Period

  • After entry, hold for minimum 30 minutes — don't watch constantly, walk away
  • NK tested 10-min, 30-min, 1-hr, 2-hr, 3-hr → 30 minutes is ideal for 5-min TF entries
  • Volume is highest during this 30-min window
  • After 30 min: plan exit or partial exit
  • Expiry day exception: Can't leave for 30 min — must be active. Do 50% exit + ride 50% with SL, because premium melts fast on expiry

Rule 4: Strike Selection by Premium Range

NK gives specific premium ranges from his R&D:

Index Premium Range (₹) Notes
Sensex ₹100 – ₹500 For OTM strikes
Nifty ₹250 – ₹800 For OTM strikes

How to use

  1. Put 4 strikes on screen (2 CE + 2 PE, OTM)
  2. If watching ₹100 premium → it will show ₹500-700 on the move
  3. If watching ₹500 premium → it will show ₹1500-2500 on the move
  4. ₹25 premium → becomes ₹75-100 on single 5-min candle
  5. All strikes on that side move together — if one doubles, they all roughly double

Strike selection principle

"Such a strike select karo ki agar SL bhi chale jaye to kam se kam loss ho." (Select a strike where even if SL hits, the loss is minimal.)

  • Find the strike with ₹100 premium in the OTM zone
  • If ₹100 premium already moved to ₹400 → don't chase it, find the next strike still at ₹50-60
  • That ₹50-60 strike will become ₹100 when the move hits it
  • Don't chase a strike that has already moved 4x

Rule 5: The "Don't Be Afraid to Buy at 300" Principle

NK addresses the psychological trap: 1. You see an option at ₹100 → hesitate (SL will be ₹40-50, scary) 2. It jumps to ₹300-400 in front of your eyes → now you can't buy (SL too big) 3. It runs to ₹1200-1500 → you just watched it

NK's solution

  • If you know the VIX + structure rules, you won't be afraid at ₹300
  • Because you know ₹300 → ₹1300 is possible (verified by the rules)
  • Verification method: Check India VIX + OTM option behavior on 5-min chart
  • If VIX broke day-high on 5-min AND option broke its level → the move is real
  • Start with small quantity, build confidence, then increase size

Rule 6: GTT Orders for Away-from-Screen

  • Place GTT (Good-Till-Triggered) orders at 3x-4x the current premium
  • Example: ₹100 option → place GTT buy at ₹230-240, SL at ₹50
  • OTM premium doesn't go easily → if it moves, it moves fast
  • GTT catches the move even if you're not watching
  • If the option moves slowly (not on 5-min) → exit, don't wait for ₹200-250

Rule 7: News Event Trading

When sudden news hits a running market: - ₹10-20 options can jump to ₹200-300 - ₹50-100 options can jump to ₹1000-1500 - Most people take small profit and exit early — wrong - Don't take small profit if the structure (VIX + 5-min break) is valid - Verify with VIX day-high break → if confirmed, ride the full move

Rule 8: Trend Confirmation by Time-Frame-of-Cut

Price cut timeframe Trend strength Duration
Single 5-min candle Strongest Intraday full ride
10 minutes Moderate Shorter move
15 minutes Weaker May reverse
1-hour Very strong 2-3 days effect

"Single candle mein katna chahiye toh wave tez hoga. 10 min pe kata toh thodi kamzor hai. 15 min pe kata toh aur kamzor. Aur 1 hour pe kata toh 2-3 din tak effect rahega."

Worked Example (from video, Sensex)

  1. 1:45 PM — Breakdown on Sensex 5-min chart at 77,800 level
  2. Check VIX — same 1:45 timeframe, VIX breaks day-high on 5-min
  3. Check option — 77,800 PE was at ₹375, jumped to ₹685 (nearly doubled)
  4. Don't chase ₹685 → find strike still at ₹100 premium → 76,800 PE
  5. Buy at ₹119, SL at ₹30 (below candle low)
  6. Hold 30 minutes → option reaches ₹581
  7. Exit or partial exit after 30 min

Key numbers from the example

  • Entry: ₹119 | SL: ₹30 | Target: ₹581 in 30 min
  • Risk: ₹89 (119-30) | Reward: ₹462 (581-119) → RR ≈ 5.2:1
  • ATM/near-ITM options roughly double on the move

Expected Trade Frequency

  • NK estimates 10-12 such trades per year that give the big moves (300→1300)
  • Monthly: 1-2 momentum events
  • Smaller moves (300→600/700) happen more often
  • Don't force trades — wait for the VIX day-high break + 5-min close confirmation

Key new insight: VIX for Puts, HM for Calls

NK explicitly states: "India VIX sirf put ke case mein dekhenge. Call ke case mein price aur Hilega Milega dekhenge." This is a significant operational distinction not captured elsewhere in the wiki — VIX is the put-side timing tool, HM is the call-side timing tool.
