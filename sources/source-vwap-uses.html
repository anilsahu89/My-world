VWAP Uses

Source

  • File: Vwap uses.pdf
  • Instructor: Nitish Kumar (NK StockTalk)

Summary

Core use cases of VWAP in trading — intraday bias, support/resistance, option trading decisions, and the "get on the train near VWAP" philosophy.

Key Teachings

Core VWAP Rules

  • VWAP is most effective for intraday because it incorporates volume
  • In a trending market going up: VWAP curves upward — every dip toward VWAP is a buy
  • In a trending market going down: VWAP curves downward — every rise toward VWAP is a sell
  • After 12:45 PM, if you missed the move, price will come close to or touch VWAP — wait for it

VWAP with Bollinger Bands

  • BB upper & lower bands act as major resistance & support
  • If price opens at BB, wait for proper Band Blast before entry
  • When price is above VWAP and above Upper Band → wait for Band Blast confirmation
  • If price fails and comes below VWAP → next target is 20 SMA
  • 20 SMA acts as an average line that attracts price (support/resistance)
  • Breaking 20 SMA requires a big candle in fast manner

VWAP Intraday Rules

  1. Price above VWAP & sustaining → if it starts going sideways, exit buy trade
  2. Don't short immediately — price that has traded above VWAP for 2-2.5 hours won't fall easily
  3. If price moves up: Previous Resistance = Next Resistance
  4. Short only when price reverses from previous resistance & closes below VWAP
  5. SL for short = previous resistance (horizontal line)

Gap Rule

  • Whenever price gaps up or down past a resistance/support, it must sustain for 2 hours to be effective

VWAP + Highest Close Rule

  • If price backs from 20 SMA, don't buy until it closes above the day's highest close price
  • Draw horizontal line at highest close → wait for close above it
  • Even if price is above VWAP, no buy until this condition is met
