Key takeaways

Hilega Milega (refines Hilega Milega): - HM's primary job = conviction / direction, not entry timing. Built for beginners to see clear buy/sell side. - RSI period 9 (not 14): 14 lags; 9 reacts before the crowd. Tested 2/3/5/7/10/14, settled on 9/10. Shifts to 7 when market is heavily down or SRT ≤ ~0.85. - The red WMA line is a volume proxy, NOT real volume — WMA chosen because it tracks volume averages closest (tested all 11 MA types). Critical on Nifty/index futures where volume is unreliable. - Planned migration to VMA (Variable Moving Average) — market structure changed post-2022 (option-selling stopped paying, algos arrived). 1–2 years out; HM still works. - RSI 86 = universal profit-booking level. Any timeframe — book and exit when RSI hits 86. After 86, RSI won't immediately re-hit; expect a pullback/consolidation before trend resumes. - For swing survival through that pullback: SMA 100 on 1-hour TF (or 50/63) protects and marks re-entry. - Does NOT work in range-bound markets — and no momentum indicator does. Larger TF = higher accuracy; below 5m/10m needs expertise. - HM is a crutch — a truly advanced trader's screen has no indicator. Success = gradually dropping indicators.

SRT (refines Srt Investing): - SRT = price ÷ 124-day average. Nifty oscillates 0.55–1.5. - < 0.6 (esp. ~0.55): lifetime wealth zone — never short. ~0.85: Nifty ~17% off top = positional BUY (hold ~6 months). Weekly > 1.24: exit equities → gold/dividend/cash. - Always check daily, not monthly (monthly misleads at ~1.53). - Nifty drawdown ladder: 5% → 12% → 17% → 36–38% (break = crash to 50–58%).

Volume (new concept — Nk Volume Reading): - 50% price + 50% volume — NK's exact split of technical analysis. Big money can hide in price/indicators/OI but cannot hide in volume. - Two-candle-close rule: never act on a breakout/breakdown without 2 closes above the level on your TF. Stated as the single biggest source of retail losses. - Breakout volume must be substantially above the surrounding 10–15–20 candles, rising. For lifetime/52-week-highs, check daily → weekly → monthly. - Bike analogy: volume = handlebar, price = bike. Volume one way, price the other = deception. - Volume + candle size must match — huge volume + tiny candle = red flag.

Options / discipline (refines Options, Psychology): - Two golden rules: (1) Intraday → read volume. (2) Swing → follow HM on ≥ 3-hour TF. - 5–7 trades/month max for option buyers. "Less trade, best trade." - April effect: tax-loss harvesting Mar 20–30 → selling stops → 9 of 10 times market up by Apr 15. - Nifty rebalancing days break the option→future→spot chain — work in buyer's favor. - Exit is everything (Abhimanyu analogy). Book loss in full + immediately; book profit in two halves. "First loss is the smallest loss." - Bull markets build false belief (even donkeys feel skilled); bear markets steal your belief. Buy when SRT < 0.6 (the village has nothing).

What NK de-emphasizes: - OI/PCR overrated — better to look at OI percentile. FII/DII cash data + IPO data underused. - Indicators should disappear as you advance. "Gut feeling has zero value in the market."
