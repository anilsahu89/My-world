Intraday Process

Intraday Process

Definition

The intraday process is the wiki's top-down decision flow for daily trading: read market direction first, pick aligned stocks, wait for VWAP/20 SMA location, confirm with HM/BB, then manage exits on 10-minute candles.

Pre-Trade Filter

  1. Avoid first 2 hours; preferred trading starts after 12:15 PM.
  2. Check Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex direction.
  3. Skip unclear direction.
  4. Classify market as rangebound or trending.
  5. Use top gainers in bullish market and top losers in bearish market.
  6. Check advance/decline.

Required Chart Setup

  • HM system.
  • Bollinger Bands with 20 SMA.
  • VWAP.
  • 9 SMA high.
  • 30-minute chart for setup observation.
  • 10-minute chart for ride/exit.

Long Setup

  • BB blast upside or wide-open BB with price behavior supporting trend.
  • Price breaks VWAP, comes near 20 SMA, bounces, and closes above VWAP.
  • HM buy.
  • RSI above 60.
  • Entry near 20 SMA / just above VWAP.
  • SL: two 10-minute closes below 20 SMA.
  • Trail/ride above 9 SMA high.

Short Setup

  • Mirror setup.
  • HM sell.
  • RSI below 45.
  • Entry near 20 SMA / just below VWAP.
  • SL: two 10-minute closes above 20 SMA.
  • Trail/ride below 9 SMA high.

OH-OL / BB Trap Branch

  • Open=High + top loser supports bearish trade.
  • Open=Low + top gainer supports bullish trade.
  • Gap-down BB Trap: only bullish side after VWAP reclaim.
  • Gap-up BB Trap: only bearish side after VWAP loss.
  • No OH/OL: use 15-minute ORB, then enter near VWAP.

Backtest Specification

  • Start with cash/futures signal first, then options overlay later.
  • Use 30-minute setup and 10-minute exit.
  • Test after-12:15 filter versus all-day variant.
  • Track index alignment, top gainer/loser rank, HM/RSI condition, VWAP/20 SMA proximity.
