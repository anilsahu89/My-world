Moving Averages (MA)

Definition

A moving average smooths price data by creating a constantly updated average price over a specific period. Used for trend identification, support/resistance, and entry/exit signals.

Types Used by NK

TypeFull NameNK Usage

WMAWeighted Moving Average3 WMA & 21 WMA on RSI (HM indicator)

COMA (Correct Order of Moving Averages)

  • 5 EMAs: 20, 50, 100, 150, 200
  • Correct order (bullish): 20 > 50 > 100 > 150 > 200 (price above all)
  • Convergence of all MAs + COMA alignment = strong trend confirmed
  • COMA + Hilega Milega signal = entry trigger
  • If MAs are tangled/mixed order → no clear trend → avoid

20 SMA — NK's Workhorse

  • Primary trend indicator on 1-hour chart
  • Above 20 SMA = uptrend, below = downtrend
  • Acts as magnet — price attracted to it
  • Breaking 20 SMA requires big candle in fast manner
  • If price backs from 20 SMA → don't buy until close above day's highest close
  • BB center line = 20 SMA (Bollinger Bands built on 20 SMA)

Moving Average Rules

  • MAs tell you direction, not exact entry
  • Combine with COMA for stronger conviction
  • Longer MA (200) = stronger S/R than shorter MA (20)
  • Golden Cross (50 above 200) = bullish signal
  • Death Cross (50 below 200) = bearish signal
  • Crossover alone is not enough — need COMA alignment + volume + HM
