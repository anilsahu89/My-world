OH-OL (Open High / Open Low)

OH-OL (Open High / Open Low)

Definition

OH-OL is an intraday option selling strategy based on identifying stocks that make their day's high or low in the very first candle of the day.

  • OH (Open = High): First candle high = day's high → stock unlikely to go higher → sell CE (deep ITM)
  • OL (Open = Low): First candle low = day's low → stock unlikely to go lower → sell PE (deep ITM)

Core Philosophy

  • Treat deep ITM options as proxy futures — they move 1:1 with underlying
  • Sell hawa (extrinsic/time value) — the premium component above intrinsic value
  • Premium decays throughout the day → seller profits from time decay
  • Keep 60% capital reserved for adjustments if trade goes against you

Trading Process

  1. Identify OH/OL stocks from first candle (9:15 AM)
  2. Check VWAP direction — should confirm OH/OL signal
  3. Select deep ITM strike — high chips, low hawa
  4. Sell the option at or near VWAP level
  5. Exit when premium decays sufficiently (typically by 3:15–3:25 PM)

Key Rules

  • VWAP confirms the direction: above VWAP → sell PE, below VWAP → sell CE
  • Previous day resistance acts as resistance for next day only
  • Don't trade if no clear OH/OL signal in first candle
  • Use 1-hour chart for confirmation if not experienced

When Combined with BB + VWAP

  • OH-OL + VWAP direction = strong confirmation for option selling
  • BB Trap scenario + OH-OL = extra confidence for PE selling
  • If price opens at BB + shows OH → high probability BB Blast fail
