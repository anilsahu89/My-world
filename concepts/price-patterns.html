Price Patterns

Price Patterns

Definition

Cumulative movements of stock prices forming distinctive shapes on a chart, used to predict future price movement with potential targets.

Two Types

Continuation Patterns

Brief pause in trend → prior trend resumes. Formed on low volumes.

PatternKey FeaturesTarget
RectangleParallel S/R lines (trading range)Prior trend direction
Ascending TriangleFlat top, rising bottomBreakout upward
Descending TriangleFlat bottom, falling topBreakout downward
Symmetrical TriangleConverging trendlinesEither direction; ½ base from breakout
FlagParallel lines, 15-20 candlesPrior trend direction
PennantShort symmetrical trianglePrior trend direction

Reversal Patterns

Signal change in current trend. Larger timeframe = higher impact.

PatternShapeSignal
Head & ShouldersThree peaksBearish (neckline break)
Inverse H&SThree troughsBullish (neckline break)
Double TopM shapeBearish
Double BottomW shapeBullish
Triple TopThree failed highsBearish
Triple BottomThree failed lowsBullish

Universal Rules

  • Volume confirms breakout — high volume on breakout = stronger signal
  • Price gap on breakout adds conviction
  • Larger timeframe = greater impact — topping patterns shorter, bottoming takes longer
  • Triangle target: Breakout at 2/3rd length; target = ½ base from breakout
  • H&S target: Distance from head peak to neckline projected below neckline
