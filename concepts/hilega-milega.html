Hilega Milega (HM)

Hilega Milega (HM)

Definition

NK's proprietary composite indicator built on RSI(9) with three overlays: - 21-period Weighted MA on RSI → the red line (Volume) - 3-period Weighted MA on RSI → the green line (Price) - RSI(9) itself (set to OB=50, OS=50 instead of default 14/70/30) → the black line (Strength) - Plus VWAP on the price chart.

"हिलेगा तो मिलेगा" — if it goes down, it will go up — the name reflects its trend-reversal signalling nature.

Primary purpose (refined Jul 2026 — Source Nk Hm Podcast 2026): HM's job is conviction / direction, not entry timing. It tells a beginner which side to buy and which side to sell. Entry/exit still come from your own skill + price structure (VWAP, MA). It is deliberately simple so newcomers see clear direction instead of "everything looks green/red."

How to set up (Zerodha Kite)

  1. Add RSI → set period to 9, Overbought=50, Oversold=50
  2. Add Weighted MA → period 21, field = RSI(9) → red line
  3. Add Weighted MA → period 3, field = RSI(9) → green line
  4. Add VWAP on price chart

Setup refinements (Jul 2026 podcast)

  • Why RSI(9), not 14: RSI(14) lags; 9 reacts before the crowd (which all use 14). NK tested 2/3/5/7/10/14 and settled on 9/10.
  • Conditional RSI(7): when the market is heavily down OR SRT ≤ ~0.85, shift HM to period 7 for faster response.
  • The red line is a volume proxy, not real volume. WMA was chosen (over all 11 MA types) because it tracks volume averages closest. Critical on Nifty/index futures where volume is unreliable. Many users wrongly assume it is real volume — see Nk Volume Reading.
  • Planned migration to VMA: NK is researching shifting the overlay to Variable Moving Average (market structure changed post-2022). 1–2 years out; HM still works.
  • Broker caveat: many brokers don't allow an indicator-on-indicator overlay — a practical setup problem.

Reading the signals

Signal Meaning
Red below Black and Blue BUY zone
Red above Green and Black SELL zone
Green above Black SELL
Below water (Blue / RSI below 50) SELL
Red line below Black → price stops going down Selling exhaustion
Gap between Green and Black Market won't go up
Red line crosses below Black line Stop selling, start buying
Black 20 SMA turns upward + candle above Stop selling

Trading rules refined (Jul 2026 podcast)

  • Stay buy-biased while red (WMA) line stays below the RSI line — ignore even a textbook price breakdown (close below prior low) if the red line is still under RSI. Price can "do whatever drama."
  • Sell/exit trigger: red line crosses above RSI and sustains (esp. with divergence forming) → expect a fall → book profit on your TF.
  • RSI 86 = universal profit-booking level. Any timeframe — when RSI hits 86, book and exit. After 86, RSI won't immediately re-hit; expect pullback/consolidation, then trend resumes.
  • Swing survival through the post-86 pullback: ride SMA 100 on 1-hour TF (or 50/63) — it protects and marks re-entry.
  • RSI 55 false-signal filter: raise the mid-line from 50 to 55; only buy when the HM curve completes a V-shape AND RSI > 55.
  • Old-low-break stat: when HM flips to buy then immediately flips back to sell, the prior swing low breaks "very badly/fast" — backtested ~10 times, works 8/10.

Timeframe hierarchy

  • Positional: analyze on Day/Week/Month, execute on 1-hr/Day
  • Intraday: analyze on 1-hr/Day, execute on 15-min/30-min
  • Swing: use ≥ 3-hour TF (going below 3h needs expertise; beginners in range-bound markets on small TFs get hurt and wrongly conclude "HM doesn't work").
  • Don't try to catch bottoms/tops.
  • 20 SMA = best support/resistance; use hourly for entry/exit. On smaller/intraday TFs use VMA (Variable Moving Average) instead of fixed SMA — it adapts to price.
  • Larger TF = higher accuracy. Below 5m/10m needs deep understanding or HM "won't work" (10-ft ladder against a 5-story building).
  • Does NOT work in range-bound markets — and no momentum indicator does. Not a flaw; a scope limit.

Critical role in the NK method

HM is the confirmation filter — used alongside Vwap, Bollinger Bands, Avwap, and COMA for every major entry. Specific callouts: - MA Convergence buy/sell rules require HM confirmation (Source Ma Convergence). - BB Trap + Three-Step pattern supersedes HM — "BB Trap and 3-step is supreme; ignore Hilega-Milega signals when both form." - OH-OL selling: confirm the sell in HM on 15-min chart (Source Oh Ol Chart Reading).

⚠️ Note

The exact indicator is not a standard library item — it's a custom Zerodha ChartIQ overlay. The interpretation rules above are from course material, not backtested. The Jul 2026 refinements (RSI 86 exit, RSI 55 filter, old-low-break 8/10, RSI 7 conditional) are not yet validated against real data — flag per CLAUDE.md.

HM is a crutch. A truly advanced trader's screen has no indicator — not even a line. Anyone trading with "just two lines" is intermediate, not pro. Success = gradually dropping indicators as skill grows. (Source Nk Hm Podcast 2026)
