Options

Options

Definition

A derivative contract giving the holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy (Call) or sell (Put) an underlying at a fixed strike price on/before expiry. You pay a premium for this right; the premium decays to zero by expiry. Calls = "चढेगा" (up view); Puts = "पडेगा" (down view).

What you actually pay for

Option premium = Intrinsic Value (chips) + Time Value (hawa). See that page for the formulas and the NK house rule: buy chips (ITM), sell hawa (OTM).

Strike classification (relative to current price)

Type CE PE Contains
ATM strike ≈ spot strike ≈ spot mostly hawa
ITM strike below spot strike above spot chips + hawa (deeper = more chips)
OTM strike above spot strike below spot only hawa → goes to 0 on expiry

⚠️ Critical: the option price is driven by the future price of the underlying, NOT the spot — except on expiry day after 12 noon, when spot takes over.

Key mechanics

  • Expiry: index options — weekly (every Thursday) + monthly (last Thursday); stock options — monthly only. If last Thursday is a holiday → previous trading day.
  • Premium decay: from purchase to expiry the premium keeps declining toward zero (theta). Weekly OTM ≈ premium / 5 per day (the 18-hour game).
  • European-style in India (NSE symbols end in "E", e.g. NIFTY 14TH JAN 13000 CE) — exercisable only on expiry.
  • Buying vs selling capital: buying 1 ATM lot = pay the premium (small); selling 1 ATM lot = pay ~future-equivalent margin (large).

Behaviour with VIX

  • Higher/rising VIX → rising premium → good for buyers.
  • Lower/falling VIX → falling premium → good for sellers.
  • Never sell options when VIX is high. Never buy OTM when VIX is falling.

Risk framing

  • Option buyer: max loss = premium paid; gains can be multifold; capital erodes fast via theta.
  • Option seller: max gain = premium; theoretically unlimited risk; needs large margin.
  • → This asymmetry is why NK hedges: combine buys + sells to cap risk and reduce margin.

Probability of Profit (POP) & Delta as POP Proxy

From Ravindra Elicherla (EP-73): sell options only when the Probability of Profit (POP) is clearly on your side — target ≥70-80% POP via far-OTM strikes. Don't chase premium size; prefer high-probability, small-premium, repeatable trades.

Delta ≈ probability of expiring ITM. So for a seller, POP ≈ 1 − delta of the sold option:

Sold option delta Approx POP (seller) Read
0.50 (ATM) ~50% Coin flip — avoid for selling
0.30 ~70% Minimum threshold for income selling
0.20 ~80% Ravindra's sweet spot
0.10 ~90% Very safe, but tiny premium

This is why Rita's Daily Theta uses 300 OTM (≈0.10-0.15 delta → ~85-90% POP) and gets 92.9% WR. The rule is now explicit: pick strike by target delta, not by premium amount.

Strategy Selection by Regime

Options strategies are not regime-independent (except Arbitrage 2.0). Before selling premium, classify the regime first — see Regime Mapping. Bull put spreads belong in uptrends; in downtrends they become serial losers.

NK's option-buyer discipline (Jul 2026 podcast)

"If you trade every day, you cannot make money by buying options."

  • 5–7 trades/month maximum for option buyers. "Less trade, best trade." More trades mainly benefit the broker and the government (STT/stamp duty = your "sleeping partner").
  • The two golden rules (NK's exact framing): 1. Intraday → read volume (Nk Volume Reading). 2. Swing → follow Hilega Milega on ≥ 3-hour TF.
  • Don't drop the mindset first: "I have ₹1L, I want ₹4L/year" — discard this. Treat trading like a business, not a multiple-X target.
  • Calendar edges for buyers:
  • April effect: tax-loss harvesting Mar 20–30 → selling stops → 9 of 10 times market up by Apr 15. Position for this up-move.
  • Nifty rebalancing days: break the option→future→spot chain — work in the buyer's favor.
  • Provable, not opinion: a low-frequency trader (50% capital, fewer trades) beats a daily trader on year-end ROI. SEBI bars NK from running the contest publicly.

⚠️ These are NK's teaching claims, not independently backtested here. The April effect and 5–7-trades rule are concrete and testable.
