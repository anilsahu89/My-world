Candlestick Patterns

Definition

Candlestick patterns are single or multi-candle formations that provide visual clues about market sentiment and potential reversals or continuations.

NK's 21 Key Candlesticks

NK teaches 21 candlestick patterns as part of chart reading. Full list in source document.

Important NK Candlestick Concepts

Inverted Hammer (in BB Trap context)

  • Long inverted candle formed outside upper BB → potential BB Trap
  • If price fails to cut the high of this candle → SL hunting coming
  • If price leaves the band and trades at same level → exit (SL hunting)

5-Candle Rule

  • If 5+ candles form at the same level, market will likely reverse
  • Applicable when price stalls at support/resistance

Big Candle Rule

  • Breaking 20 SMA requires a big candle in a fast manner
  • Similarly, trend reversals come with large-bodied candles
  • Small candles at key levels = indecision → wait for confirmation

Moh Maya Candle

  • Single candle forms outside lower Bollinger Band
  • Next day price trades above VWAP on hourly chart → buy signal
  • See Moh Maya for full strategy

Candlestick Usage in NK System

  • Candlesticks are confirmation tools, not standalone signals
  • Always combine with:
  • VWAP for bias
  • BB for context (inside/outside band)
  • HM for timing (strength direction)
  • COMA for trend alignment
  • First candle of the day is critical for OH-OL identification
