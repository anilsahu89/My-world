VIX (Volatility Index)

VIX (Volatility Index)

Definition

India VIX = the volatility index, measuring expected near-term volatility of Nifty. Add it to your watchlist. Lower VIX = lower volatility; higher VIX = higher volatility. The hawa component of every option moves with VIX.

The core relationship

> Index option price is directly correlated to India VIX.

VIXPremiumFavourable for
Rising / highrising premium (added fast to OTM)Buyers
Falling / lowfalling premium (OTM loses fast)Sellers
  • Never sell an option when VIX is high.
  • Never buy OTM when VIX is falling — the option loses value with no market move.

VIX as a trading signal (9 SMA rule)

From Source Option Calculation:

  • Plot 9 SMA of High on the VIX chart, 1-hour TF.
  • Once VIX closes above 9 SMA → it shoots up.
  • Once VIX closes below 9 SMA → it falls.
  • "Once VIX comes above 9 SMA it shoots and vice versa."

VIX-dependent strike selection

The NK half-hedge table (Chips And Hawa) is indexed by VIX band — wider offsets when VIX is 18–22, tighter when below 18. VIX literally decides which strikes to trade.

VIX + AVWAP option-selling zone

Sell OTM put (uptrend) / OTM call (downtrend) when VIX is below AVWAP on the hourly chart (Source Option Calculation). "This kind of option selling gives a lot of money."

VIX as intraday timing trigger (5-min TF)

From Source Vix Intraday 5Min — NK's dedicated intraday option-buying framework:

VIX for Puts, HM for Calls (key operational split)

> "India VIX sirf put ke case mein dekhenge. Call ke case mein price aur Hilega Milega dekhenge."

  • PUT buying: Check India VIX (day-high break on 5-min = trigger)
  • CALL buying: Check price action + Hilega Milega (don't check VIX)
  • This is a significant operational distinction — VIX is the put-side timing tool, HM is the call-side equivalent

Gap + VIX Direction (opening setup)

ScenarioVIX ConditionMeaningAction
Gap UpVIX down >4%Gap sustainsBuy after 5-min close, SL = day low
Gap DownVIX up >4%Gap sustainsSell after 5-min close, SL = day high
  • 4% VIX threshold is the filter — below 4%, signal is weaker
  • Wait for 5-min candle close (closing-basis SL, not touch)

VIX Day-High Break = Wave Strength

  • On 5-min chart, mark VIX day-high
  • VIX breaks day-high in single 5-min candle → strong, sustainable move (full ride)
  • Break in 10 min → weaker (300→600/700)
  • Break in 15 min → even weaker
  • Break on 1-hour TF → very strong, effect lasts 2-3 days
  • If VIX does NOT break day-high → wave is weak, can reverse anytime

30-Minute Holding Rule

  • After entry, hold minimum 30 minutes (don't watch constantly)
  • NK tested 10-min, 30-min, 1-hr, 2-hr, 3-hr → 30 min is ideal for 5-min entries
  • Expiry day exception: be active, do 50% exit + ride 50% with SL

Strike Selection by Premium Range

IndexPremium Range (₹)
Sensex₹100 – ₹500

  • Find OTM strike at ₹100 premium → becomes ₹400-700 on the move
  • Don't chase a strike that already moved 4x → find next strike still at ₹50-60
  • All strikes on that side move together — if one doubles, they all roughly double

> ⚠️ The VIX day-high break trigger and 30-min hold rule are untested against Rita's data — candidates for backtesting.
