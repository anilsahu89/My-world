Concept: VPA (Volume Price Analysis)

Concept: VPA (Volume Price Analysis)

Definition

VPA = Volume Price Analysis. Reading who is in control of a market — buyers or sellers — by studying the relationship between effort (volume) and result (price spread / candle direction). Foundation: Anna Coulling's A Complete Guide to Volume Price Analysis and the Wyckoff Law of Effort vs Result. See Source Anna Coulling Vpa.

VPA is the umbrella framework. The specific single-bar alert lives in Volume Mismatch; the tradeable strategy lives in Vpa. This page is the higher-level concept.

⭐ The Master Rule (Rita's version, 2026-07-23)

> Volume is the KING. Do NOT go by candle color blindly. Rising volume is the criterion.

When volume is rising/added but price is NOT moving up (price flat, or even slightly red) → "something is cooking" → smart money is accumulating → price will move UP.

  • Volume leads. Price follows. Volume is the truth-teller because it cannot be faked; candle direction can.
  • Candle color is secondary confirmation — useful once you see the volume signal, but never the trigger on its own.
  • The chart signal Rita identified: rising volume appearing against a flat/down price → accumulation → next candle goes green and confirms the move up.

Volume Color Definition (locked)

LabelDefinitionCode
Green volumeVolume rising vs the previous candle (added / surging volume)vol[t] > vol[t-1]
Red volumeVolume falling vs the previous candle (declining volume)vol[t] < vol[t-1]
Strong green volumeVolume ≥ 1.5× previous candle (smart-money footprint)vol[t] ≥ 1.5 × vol[t-1]

> ⚠️ Volume color is NOT tied to whether the candle closed up or down. It is purely relative volume (today vs yesterday). This is the source of most VPA confusion — green volume can appear on a red candle, and that mismatch IS the signal.

The 3-Candle Confirmation Pattern (the actionable rule)

The single-bar table in Volume Mismatch is the raw alert. For a tradeable entry, use the 3-candle confirmation:

Long (bullish) confirmation

DayCandleVolumeRead
Day 1Red (down)Red (falling)Sellers exhausting — down move has no fuel
Day 2Green (up, close > open)Green (rising, ≥1.5× Day 1)CONFIRMATION — volume added, price follows → enter long

Logic: Day 1's red candle on falling volume = weak selling. Day 2's rising volume with a green close proves buyers have taken control. The added volume on Day 2 is the proof that "something was cooking."

Short (bearish) confirmation — mirror

DayCandleVolumeRead
Day 1Green (up)Red (falling)Buyers exhausting — up move has no fuel
Day 2Red (down, close < open)Green (rising, ≥1.5× Day 1)CONFIRMATION — distribution confirmed → enter short

Effort vs Result (Wyckoff / Coulling framework)

The theoretical backbone of all VPA signals:

Effort (Volume)Result (Price Spread)Interpretation
HighLarge✅ Genuine move — trend likely to continue
HighSmall⚠️ Volume mismatch — high effort, weak result → reversal likely
LowLargeSuspicious — may not sustain
LowSmallNormal — no signal

> "High effort + small result" is the classical reversal tell: big volume pushed, but price barely moved. Smart money was on the other side of the trade.

How VPA is Used

VPA is dual-purpose — it works standalone AND as a confirmation filter inside other strategies:

Breakout validityAny breakout candle must show rising volume — flat/falling volume on a breakout = suspect

Smart Money Footprint

  • High volume = institutions / smart money are active. Retail alone cannot generate surging volume.
  • If high volume can't push price through a level → they are selling into strength (at upper band / resistance) or buying into weakness (at lower band / support).
  • Volume reveals intent that price hides. This is the core of Anna Coulling's framework — see Source Anna Coulling Vpa.

Works on Any Timeframe

Rita's requirement: VPA applies on any TF — 1m, 5m, 15m, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly. The 3-candle pattern and effort-vs-result logic are timeframe-agnostic.

  • Swing trades: daily candles (default)
  • Intraday: 15-min candles
  • Investment bias: weekly / monthly

Backtest Candidates

  1. 3-candle confirmation hit rate — % of Day1 red+red → Day2 green+green (≥1.5× vol) setups that move up over next N days.
  2. Volume multiple tuning — is 1.5× the right threshold, or 2×? Test win rate vs trade frequency.
  3. Context filter value — does requiring the setup near BB lower band (long) / upper band (short) materially lift win rate?
  4. Timeframe consistency — does the pattern work equally on daily vs 15-min?
  5. VPA as BB Trap filter — does requiring no bearish mismatch on breakout candles reduce false traps? (Already partly tested in BB Trap v2.)

> Status: rule defined, NOT yet backtested. A dedicated VPA backtester is a separate pending task.

Limitations

  • No numeric volume threshold is "officially" correct — 1.5× is a reasonable starting point borrowed from the BB Trap convention; needs empirical tuning.
  • VPA on very low-volume stocks is unreliable — smart-money footprint is hard to read when overall liquidity is thin.
  • Real-time volume (intraday) can be noisy near the open/close; the 3-candle pattern is cleaner on completed candles.
  • "Rising volume" alone is not enough — it must be paired with the price-spread read (effort vs result) to avoid false signals.
