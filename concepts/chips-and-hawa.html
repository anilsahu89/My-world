Chips & Hawa (Intrinsic vs Time Value)

Definition

NK's house vocabulary for option pricing:

  • Chips = Intrinsic Value — the real, "yours to keep" portion.
  • Hawa = Time Value (literally "air") — the speculative portion that decays to zero by expiry.

> "जो चिप्स है वो अपना है, जो हवा है वो सपना है" — chips are yours, hawa is a dream.

Formulas


For a Call:   Chips = Spot − Strike
For a Put:    Chips = Strike − Spot
For both:     Hawa  = Premium − Chips

⚠️ Nuance: chips/hawa are defined using spot price, but the option's behaviour follows the future price. (Options)

Fast mental calc (rule of thumb)

Spot − nearest ATM gives the rough "chips width" per 100 points. E.g. spot 14507, ATM 14500 → 14500−14200 = 300 → a 14200 CE has ~300 chips. ~90–100% accuracy.

The NK house rules (the whole point)

  1. Buyers buy chips (ITM), never OTM. ITM behaves like the future — minimal time decay. OTM is pure hawa that dissolves.
  2. Sellers sell hawa (OTM), never ITM. You collect the hawa as profit; it goes to zero on expiry.
  3. Hedge pattern: buy ITM (pay chips+hawa) + sell far OTM (collect 100% hawa) → effective cost drops, breakeven shifts favorably. Worked examples in Source Option Basics (BN @31600, bullish & bearish).
  4. Hawa only pays off after a BB Blast.

Why this concept underpins everything

  • Every hedge in the wiki is fundamentally a chips/hawa engineering exercise: maximize chips retained, minimize hawa paid, sell hawa to fund chips.
  • The "18-hour game" (weekly theta) and rangebound plays are pure hawa-decay exploitation.
