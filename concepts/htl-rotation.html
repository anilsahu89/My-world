Concept: HTL Rotation (Gold ↔ Nifty Fund-Flow Switch)

Definition

HTL Rotation is the regime switch popularized by the HT Legend 6-year ledger video: when money flows out of Nifty/equities into gold, shift the option-writing book from index premium to gold premium (and back when the flow reverses). It is a two-asset specialization of regime mapping — instead of adjusting strategy parameters per regime, you change the underlying you sell premium on, keeping the theta-decay engine constant while the market decides which asset pays.

Named so the vault has one handle for the idea. Mechanics are the source's claim, not our validation — see HTL Thread for the running comparison.

Claimed mechanics (per the source — unverified)

  • A green/red fund-flow dial between gold and Nifty: green = money rotating into gold from other asset classes → trade gold; flow back into Nifty → trade index.
  • Claimed gold era: ~Oct 2024 – Apr 2026; claimed distribution signal: Nifty rose 24k → ~25k in early 2025 while funds flowed out.
  • Entries near candle lows (pullback entries within a flow-confirmed direction), hedged option writing, most positions covered before expiry, profit mostly theta.
  • Claimed output: ~₹1.06 Cr on gold in ~18 months, "8–9%/month" on deployed capital.

Why it matters to us

  • Convergent independent witness: NK's srt investing routes to gold independently — weekly SRT > 1.24 → exit equities → gold / dividend / cash. Two unconnected teachers point at the same equity-extreme → gold rotation.
  • Matches our own tape: Sep-2026 paper window — NIFTY grinding down, O=H shorts winning / O=L longs bleeding, gold at highs. The rotation direction is what our live book has been screaming.
  • Already have the plumbing: the goldcrypto paper engine trades GC=F daily; our validated edges (Open=Low intraday PF 4.25, BB Trap v2 shorts PF 2.10) can be re-run gated by this switch to measure the lift, if any.

Testable proxy (backtest candidate #0 in regime mapping)

  • Flow dial proxy: gold/Nifty relative strength — e.g., 50-day slope or 12/26-week RS of GC=F (or MCX gold in INR) vs NIFTY 50.
  • Question to answer: does gating our Nifty premium engines OFF and gold premium ON during "funds-out-of-Nifty" windows improve aggregate PF vs ungated?
  • Cheap first cut: classify the last ~3 years into gold-era / nifty-era windows by RS, overlay our existing backtest month-P&Ls, compare.

Hard caveats

  • The exact fund-flow formula was never disclosed — any proxy we test is ours, not theirs.
  • The 8–9%/month gold claim is unaudited marketing; only the rotation direction is being adopted for testing, nothing else.
  • Gold in INR has a currency component (USD/INR) a Nifty-only habit tends to forget — factor it in any backtest.
