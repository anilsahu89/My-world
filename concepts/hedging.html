Hedging

Hedging

Definition

A technique to ensure a market position is not wiped out by an adverse move — construct a portfolio (futures + options, or options + options) so that one leg's loss is offset by another's gain. NK's metaphor: "erecting a fence around your farm." More importantly: a thought process that flexes with the market.

Why hedge (the structural reason)

  • Margin reduction: a hedged combo needs far less margin than naked legs. E.g. SBI Jan future alone = ₹3,02,591; the matching put alone = ₹54,450; combined-hedged = ₹84,611 (≈ intraday margin) — "like magic."
  • Risk capping: you can compute max loss before entry.
  • Survival: "First loss is always the smallest." Hedging lets you make the right decision when a trade goes wrong instead of panicking.

Market-participant framing (NK's worldview)

  • Speculators (~4%) — Rakesh Jhunjhunwala / Buffett types
  • Hedgers (~5%) — FIIs, DIIs, smart money ← who actually makes money
  • Arbitrageurs (~92%) — common investors, who lose to the hedgers

Three types of risk (from Source Hedge 23 / Hedge 23 class)

  1. Known risk — you know your risk and reward upfront.
  2. Unknown risk — trade taken without study (tips, TV, news); you don't know what can happen.
  3. Planned risk — risk you plan before entry, then minimize/break-even/zero it after entry. Plan Risk = Hedge = Insurance. "क्या Hedge करने वाले 100% Success…" (the rhetorical question: do hedgers win 100%? — no, but the math favors them).

The universal hedge pattern (chips/hawa view)

Buy chips (ITM) for directional exposure → sell hawa (far OTM) to discount the purchase → net cost drops, breakeven shifts favorably, max loss is bounded. See worked examples in Source Option Basics and Source Option Calculation.

Adjustment (the discipline)

Hedges are not set-and-forget: roll legs as price moves (e.g. Airtel future hedge: roll 570 PE → 530 PE, 580 CE → 550 CE on a fall; sell an extra call for extra profit in a downtrend). Book the long leg when it reaches 95% price + 5% chips, or when its liquidity dries up.

When NOT to hedge / when to exit

  • If trend reverses against a multi-leg position and it looks like a trap → exit all positions and rebuild for the new conditions (Source Option Calculation).
  • "First loss is always the smallest" — don't widen the timeframe to avoid booking it.
