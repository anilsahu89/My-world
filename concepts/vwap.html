VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)

VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)

Definition

VWAP = cumulative (price × volume) / cumulative volume — reset daily. It represents the "fair price" where the average participant has traded.

Core Principle

> "Get on the train near VWAP" — VWAP is the station; price keeps returning to it.

Key Rules

Trend Direction

  • VWAP curving up + price above VWAP → bullish → every dip to VWAP = buy opportunity
  • VWAP curving down + price below VWAP → bearish → every rise to VWAP = sell opportunity
  • VWAP flat → sideways market → avoid directional trades

Intraday Behavior

  • Most effective for intraday trading (incorporates volume)
  • After 12:45 PM, price tends to revert toward VWAP if a move was missed earlier
  • Price that stays above VWAP for 2–2.5 hours has strong bullish bias — don't short casually

Support & Resistance

  • VWAP acts as dynamic S/R level throughout the day
  • When combined with Bollinger Bands:
  • Upper/Lower bands = major S/R
  • Price above VWAP + above Upper Band → wait for Band Blast
  • Price failing below VWAP → next target = 20 SMA
  • Previous day resistance acts as resistance for the next day only

2-Hour Sustain Rule

  • If price gaps up/down past a resistance/support, it must sustain for 2 hours to confirm the breakout

20 SMA Interaction

  • 20 SMA attracts price like a magnet (acts as S/R)
  • Breaking 20 SMA requires a big candle in fast manner
  • If price backs from 20 SMA, don't buy until it closes above the day's highest close

VWAP with Options

  • For OH-OL option selling: use VWAP as the bias indicator
  • Price above VWAP → sell PE (bearish on put)
  • Price below VWAP → sell CE (bullish on call)
