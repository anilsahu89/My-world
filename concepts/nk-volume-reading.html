NK's Volume Reading (50/50 thesis)

Definition

NK sir's own framing of volume as the one footprint big money cannot fake. His exact split: technical analysis = 50% price action + 50% volume. Big money (operators/FIs) can deceive you in price, indicators, and OI — but not in volume, because every real transaction leaves a real size.

This is distinct from Vpa (Anna Coulling's effort-vs-result framework, which NK studied as a foundation) and Volume Mismatch (the single-bar signal). NK's version centers on breakout confirmation and the price-volume direction agreement.

The two-candle-close law (NK's #1 rule)

> Never act on a breakout/breakdown without two candle closes above/below the level on the timeframe you trade.

Stated as the single biggest source of retail losses — bigger than any strategy flaw. Breakout/breakdown levels hold the most liquidity, which is exactly where big money distributes to retail. NK admits he lost money on the Nifty ATH breakout (26,061, Sep 2024 broken Nov–Dec 2025) by skipping this.

Breakout volume rules

  • Breakout volume must be substantially higher than the surrounding 10–15–20 candles AND in a rising state. No fixed multiplier — "kaafi zyada."
  • For lifetime-high / 52-week-high breakouts: if not visible on daily, check weekly; if not weekly, check monthly. On at least one TF the volume must clearly exceed the surrounding average.
  • Volume + candle size must match. Huge volume + tiny candle body = red flag — question the move. "Where the volume is, the price should also be there."

The bike analogy (price-volume agreement)

  • Volume = handlebar. Price = bike. Turn the handlebar left, the bike must turn left.
  • Volume one direction, price the other (or price stalls/freezes) = deception by big money. A trap.
  • This is the same principle as Vpa (effort vs result) and Volume Mismatch, restated as a directional-consistency check.

Big-money mechanics (why volume needs time)

Big money buys cheap in the "warehouse" (accumulation), then sells slowly to retail at the "stall" (distribution) — never all at once. This is why:

  • Volume needs time/confirmation (the two-close rule).
  • Breakout points are distribution traps, not free money.

How volume fits with Hilega Milega

  • Hilega Milega gives direction/conviction; volume is the confirmation layer on top, not a replacement.
  • Do NOT jump on every minor HM signal in a sideways/range market — wait for the full HM curve + V-shape + volume confirmation.
  • NK's plan (in progress, not yet released): integrate real-time volume layers — volume smash, stopping volume, sign volume — with HM + ORB.

What NK de-emphasizes

  • No indicator (lagging or leading) tells you an old low will break — HM + volume does. Indicators "cheat/deceive"; volume doesn't.
  • OI / PCR overrated. Better to look at OI percentile (e.g. 90th percentile = price has been here only 10% of time → expect mean reversion). Nobody talks about percentile.
  • FII/DII cash data + IPO data are useful but underused vs the hype around OI.
  • Closed-market hindsight is misleading — "anyone can show buy here/sell here after the fact."
