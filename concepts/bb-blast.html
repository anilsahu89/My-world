BB Blast (Bollinger Band Breakout)

Definition

A continuation/breakout event: after a period of band squeeze (low volatility, narrow bands), price blasts out of a band and rides it — the start of a trending move. This is the NK pattern most analogous to what Rita's BB breakout v2 scanner hunts.

Confirmation rules (from Source Class 6 Ohol)

A BB Blast is confirmed when all four hold:

  1. Band squeeze came first (narrow bands / momentum compressed).
  2. Movement on higher TF — momentum sustains on the higher timeframe.
  3. Movement on lower TF — momentum sustains on the lower timeframe.
  4. (Implied) blast direction = the side it exits.

> If bands were not narrow and price still blasts → it's a BB Trap, not a real blast.

How to trade it

  • Intraday (15-min, Source Ma Convergence): buy only above 20 SMA (the middle band), sell only below; confirm with Vwap + Hilega Milega. Stay in the trade while price walks the band.
  • Options (Source Option Calculation): on a BB Blast, buy OTM, sell further OTM (defined-risk spread).
  • Confirmation: blast + next-day gap up + sustain = confirmed real blast.
  • SL: when price closes back inside the band / below 20 SMA.

Sub-types / variations

  • Gap-down in BB Blast → "Bargain Candle" setup (per Source Class 6 Ohol).
  • BB Blast interacts with Oh Ol (Open High / Open Low) for additional context.
  • Moh Maya is a positional variant: outside-band candle + next-day VWAP reclaim.
