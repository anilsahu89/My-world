SRT Investing

Definition

SRT is a long-term investing ratio:

SRT = latest spot / 124-day SMA

124 days ≈ 6 months ≈ 50% of ~248 trading days. The source uses it to identify broad Nifty investment zones.

Rules

  • SRT between 0.6 and 0.9 is considered a strong investing zone.
  • Monthly HM should be buy before investing.
  • Add in batches:
  • 30% capital when SRT reaches 0.9.
  • Add every 0.1 downside.
  • Safe investment areas also include weekly AVWAP / 20 SMA and convergence of 20, 50, 124, 150, 200 EMA.

NK's refined thresholds (Jul 2026 podcast)

Nifty SRT normally oscillates 0.55 to 1.5. Specific zones:

SRT level Meaning Action
< 0.6 (esp. ~0.55) Lifetime wealth-creation zone Never short, even if paid to. Occurs once every 10–14 years (last: Covid 2020; Nifty "Lakshman Rekha" 7410, low 7411)
~0.85 Nifty ~17% off its top Positional BUY zone — hold ~6 months
Weekly > 1.24 Overheated Exit equities → gold / dividend-yielding / cash. Even if it runs to 1.30 (+30%), the exit was right

Critical: always check SRT on DAILY, not monthly — monthly shows ~1.53 and misleads.

Nifty drawdown ladder: 5% → 12% → 17% → 36–38%. A break of 36–38% = crash to 50–58% (as in 2008 / Covid). 17% breaks only on genuinely bad news.

Cross-instrument caveat: stock/commodity SRT levels differ from Nifty's — do NOT apply Nifty SRT levels to other instruments.

HM linkage: when SRT ≤ ~0.85, NK shifts Hilega Milega to RSI period 7 for faster response.

Backtest Specification

  • Instrument: Nifty index or Nifty ETF proxy.
  • Daily close and 124 SMA.
  • Monthly HM filter if data is available; otherwise test SRT-only first.
  • Entry ladder at 0.9, 0.8, 0.7, 0.6.
  • Exit variants: SRT mean reversion, fixed holding period, monthly HM sell.

Status

⚠️ The 0.55 / 0.85 / 1.24 thresholds and the "9–14 year recurrence" claim are NK's teaching, not independently backtested here. The drawdown ladder (5/12/17/36-38%) is testable on Nifty history — good backtest candidate.
