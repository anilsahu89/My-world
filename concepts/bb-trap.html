BB Trap (Bollinger Band Trap)

BB Trap (Bollinger Band Trap)

Definition

A reversal pattern where retailers get trapped on a fake gap move. Price gaps up/down, the first candle closes outside the Bollinger Band, the next candle re-enters and closes below VWAP — the move fails and reverses hard. "Because this traps the retailers, it's called BB Trap." It is the opposite of BB Blast (which sustains outside the band; the trap fails to sustain).

Why it works

  • Market gaps → retailers pile in at the open.
  • Smart money sells into that buying at the high.
  • Price collapses back below VWAP and stays there all day → retailers trapped.
  • "Where retailers get trapped, only there you make money."

Identification rules (from Source Bb Trap Mentorship3)

  1. Use 15-min / 30-min / 1-hour TF.
  2. Look only at scripts with a strong gap up/down open.
  3. Minimum one candle must close outside the band.
  4. Next candle comes back inside the band AND closes below the low of the outside-band candle.
  5. Following candles stay below VWAP and sustain.
  6. → Short (after confirmation) near VWAP. For options, take ATM.

BB Trap on 15-min (Bank Nifty example, Source Bb Trap Hedging)

  • 500-pt gap up; entire first candle outside upper band.
  • Next candle closes below prior candle AND below VWAP.
  • SL above the high of the prior (outside-band) candle.

Variants / intensity

  • Frequency: forms 5–6 times/month in indices, 2–3 times/month in a stock.
  • Bigger TF = bigger money.
  • Jackpot trade: BB Trap on all three TFs simultaneously (15/30/60 min) → very big move. Occurs once in 2–3 months. "Very little SL hunting in this pattern."
  • Positional BB Trap: use on daily TF. On monthly chart → signals a massive fall.

The 4 ways to trade a BB Trap (from Source Bb Trap Hedging, Indigo example)

Choose based on capital, risk tolerance, and emotion:

# Trade Notes
1 Cash intraday (MIS) Sell short in cash; MIS only, never NRML/carry
2 Short Future + hedge Sell Future + sell ATM Put simultaneously (premium income + directional)
3 Sell Call above VWAP Naked or hedged; hedge = buy a call 50 pts above the sold strike. Hedged profit smaller (~8–9 pts) but no system SL needed
4 Buy 1-strike-away OTM Put + sell far OTM Put Defined risk = buy−sell premium; e.g. risk ₹19 for the full month

Worked hedge math (Indigo, BB Trap short)

  • Entry 1672 (below the VWAP-close candle).
  • Future + Put hedge: short Indigo future + short 1600 PE @67.95 → put MTM loss ₹15.05, future +46 → net +31 pts.
  • Call spread: short 1680 CE (high 63.90) + buy 1730 CE (hedge) → capped risk, smaller profit.
  • Put spread: buy 1660 PE @67 + sell 1620 PE @48 → max risk ₹19 for the month.

Stop-loss

  • System SL: above the high of the outside-band candle.
  • Practical SL: wait for a full candle to close above VWAP before quitting.
  • If hedged: no system SL needed.

When NOT to trade a BB Trap

  • Lower-band BB Trap + Open = High → avoid.
  • Upper-band BB Trap + Open = Low → avoid.

Supreme override

"BB Trap and 3-step is supreme." If BB Trap + three-step pattern forms, ignore Hilega-Milega signals. Retailer trapping does not show on indicators until later.

How to hedge a Future in a BB Trap (Bank Nifty, Source Bb Trap Hedging)

  • Short Nifty future + buy ITM Call with ~70% chips (≈300 pts ITM) → risk per lot ≈ 10 pts, margin ≈ ₹34,000/lot. No system SL needed when hedged.
  • Or: short Future + short ATM Put (high VIX → sell put; low VIX → buy call).

Cross-check vs Rita's BB breakout v2

BB Trap is a reversal; BB breakout v2 is a continuation. They are distinct patterns but share the BB + VWAP vocabulary. When v2 is documented, confirm v2 explicitly excludes trap setups (i.e. requires the breakout to sustain, not re-enter the band) — this is exactly the trap-vs-blast distinction NK draws.
