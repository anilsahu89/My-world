Bollinger Bands

Bollinger Bands

Definition

A volatility indicator (John Bollinger): a band plotted at ±2 standard deviations around a 20-period SMA (defaults: period 20, stddev 2). Upper band = resistance, lower band = support, middle = 20 SMA. The band width expands/contracts with volatility.

Core behaviours (NK framework)

  • Squeeze → blast: when bands are narrow for a while (low volatility), a sharp directional move becomes likely. If bands were not narrow and it "blasts" → likely a trap.
  • Band walk: a strong trend can "walk" along a band — stay in the trade till price leaves the band.
  • Mean reversion inside range: price tends to bounce between bands; bounce off lower band + cross above middle → upper band is the target.
  • Breakout invalidation: if price exits a band but immediately re-enters → the breakout strength is negated.
  • Timeframe effect (from Source Class 6 Ohol): a BB event on day TF → 7–28 days effect; hourly → 2–3 days. (BB Blast duration table on Bb Blast.)
  • Confirmation: BB blast + next day gap up + sustain = confirmed.

Intraday trading rules (15-min, from Source Ma Convergence)

  • Buy only when candle is above the 20 SMA (black/middle line).
  • Sell only when candle is below 20 SMA.
  • Confirm with Vwap + Hilega Milega.
  • "Whenever it touches 20 SMA, price will jump."

Two NK signature BB patterns

  • BB Blast — the continuation/breakout (and the pattern your BB breakout v2 scanner targets).
  • BB Trap — the reversal trap (retailers get caught on a fake gap move).

BB in the broader method

  • Combined with Vwap (the "station"), Avwap, Hilega Milega (confirmation), and the 20/50/100/150/200 EMA COMA stack.
  • Moh Maya uses BB as its outside-the-band trigger.
  • Overbought Oversold is partly read via BB.
  • AVWAP "works well with Bollinger Band also."
