Hedge 17 (H-17)

Hedge 17 (H-17)

Definition

Hedge 17 exploits the correlation between Nifty and Bank Nifty. You're simultaneously: option buyer AND seller, long AND short, monthly AND weekly, Nifty AND Bank Nifty.

Core Logic

  • Many common stocks between Nifty and Bank Nifty → correlated movement
  • When Nifty goes up, Bank Nifty also goes up (usually)
  • Sometimes Nifty halts but Bank Nifty keeps going up
  • Strategy uses merit/demerit of both monthly and weekly expiries

Why "Hedge 17"?

  • Bank Nifty side sold 1700 points OTM → hence "1700" → "Hedge 17"

Execution Steps

Step 1: Buy Nifty (Monthly)

  • Buy 1 lot Monthly Expiry Nifty
  • If trading after 15th → buy next month expiry (premium erodes faster in last week)
  • Strike price not critical — buy option in range ₹80–110
  • If buying 1st–10th of month: target ₹100–115

Step 2: Sell Bank Nifty (Weekly)

  • Sell 2 lots of Bank Nifty 1500–1700 points OTM (weekly expiry)
  • If selling Friday/Monday → current week; else next week
  • Price should not exceed ₹70–90 per share
  • If can't find price range → go to next week expiry

Step 3: If Bank Nifty Doubles Intraday

  • If sold premium doubles (₹90 → ₹180) → exit one lot of Bank Nifty
  • Continue with remaining positions
  • In 80-85% of cases, price doesn't double intraday

Scenarios

Bank Nifty Goes Up 1700 Points

  • If Bank Nifty +1700, Nifty also +350-400 points minimum
  • ₹90 Nifty call → becomes ₹350-400
  • Bank Nifty never gives loss until it crosses 1700 points above strike
  • On expiry, Bank Nifty ₹90 premium → 0 → profit

Market Goes Down

  • Nifty may drop ₹90 → ₹60
  • But Bank Nifty sales become zero on expiry → earn premium
  • When Nifty premium reduces to ₹30 → exit, take new position ₹100-115 of next monthly expiry
  • Keep selling weekly expiry Bank Nifty

Key Features

  • VIX resistant — does not get impacted with VIX volatility
  • Positional trade with ₹1.5-1.6 lakh investment → 15-17% returns
  • Call side only
  • Sell 2nd lot of Bank Nifty + buy 1 lot Nifty @ ₹80-90

When Bank Nifty Goes Up 1700 Points (Risk)

  • This is the rare loss scenario
  • Need Bank Nifty to move 1700 points above strike to incur loss
  • Nifty gains compensate partially

Quick Reference

Component Action
Nifty Buy 1 lot monthly (₹80-110)
Bank Nifty Sell 2 lots weekly (₹70-90, 1700 OTM)
If BN doubles Exit 1 lot
If Nifty → ₹30 Exit, re-enter next month
Target return 15-17% on ₹1.5-1.6 lakh
