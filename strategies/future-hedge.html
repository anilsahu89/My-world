Future Hedge (H-20)

Future Hedge (H-20)

Definition

Future Hedge (H-20) = Buy Future + Buy deep ITM Put (70% chips) to define loss while keeping unlimited profit with reduced margin.

When to Use

  • When in dilemma whether market will continue trend or reverse
  • At support or resistance where market is overbought/oversold
  • When you have no clue about direction but expect momentum either side
  • Best at strength-wise resistance/support

Core Logic

  • Buy Future (unlimited upside) + Buy ITM Put (defines downside loss)
  • If market goes your way → unlimited profit
  • If market reverses → loss limited to Put cost
  • If market sideways → loss due to Put premium decay → needs momentum

Design (Bullish Example)

Initiate at Support @ 15000

  • Buy Nifty FUT @ 15000 (1 lot)
  • Buy Nifty 15500 PE @ 650 (1 lot) — 76% chips ITM

Payoff

  • Upside profit: unlimited
  • Downside loss: limited (starts from 15512 without adjustment = 16387 − 875)
  • Sideways: small loss from Put premium decay

Full Hedge (H-20 + H-23 Combined)

  • Combine Future Hedge + Hedge 23
  • Buy Future + Buy 70% chips Put + Short deep ITM 70% chips CE
  • Profit in: call side + put side + sideways/range-bound market
  • Very safe trade

Adjustment — If Support Broken

Step 1: Add Far OTM Put

  • Original: Nifty FUT buy @ 15000 + Nifty 15500 PE buy @ 650
  • Add: 14500 PE buy @ 100 (1 lot)

Step 2: If Market Reverses at 14500 → Create Spread

  • Nifty FUT buy @ 15000
  • Nifty 15500 PE buy @ 650
  • 14500 PE buy @ 100
  • 14300 PE sell @ 50 (1 lot)

Result

  • Trade protected even if market reverses
  • If trend becomes clear → pyramid sell if margin available

Key Rules

  1. Initiate at support or resistance only
  2. Needs momentum — don't use in dead sideways market
  3. Combine with H-23 for "Full Hedge" (all-direction protection)
  4. Keep margin available for adjustments
  5. 70% chips ITM Put is the protection leg
