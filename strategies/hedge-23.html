Hedge 23 (H-23)

Hedge 23 (H-23)

Definition

Hedge 23 = Selling deep ITM options (with 70%+ chips/intrinsic value) to capture the hawa (time value). The hedge gives "space" — if you're wrong, you exit cost-to-cost or with minor loss.

Core Philosophy

Buy the chips (intrinsic value), sell the hawa (time value). Deep ITM options behave like futures.

When to Execute

  • High VIX → high premium → high profit (best time)
  • Normal VIX → normal premium → less profit but still works
  • Never jump in-between — execute only near major support/resistance
  • Mark S/R on weekly + daily charts using BB (upper band = resistance, lower band = support)

Three Types of Risk (NK's Framework)

  1. Known Risk — you know your risk & reward upfront
  2. Unknown Risk — trade taken blindly (tips, TV news) — avoid
  3. Planned Risk — risk planned before entry → minimized via hedging

Planned Risk = Hedge = Insurance

Design — With Future (Positional)

Bullish View

  1. Mark major support (e.g., Bank Nifty near 35000)
  2. Buy Bank Nifty MAY FUT @ 37365
  3. Sell Bank Nifty MAY 35000 CE @ 2890 (deep ITM, near support)

Chips-Hawa Calculation: - Chips = Future Price − Strike = 37365 − 35000 = 2365 - Hawa = Premium − Chips = 2890 − 2365 = 525 - Target: Capture the 525 points of hawa

Bearish View

  1. Mark major resistance (e.g., Bank Nifty near 39000)
  2. Sell Bank Nifty MAY FUT @ 37365
  3. Sell Bank Nifty MAY 39000 PE @ 2319 (deep ITM, near resistance)

Chips-Hawa Calculation: - Chips = Strike − Future Price = 39000 − 37365 = 1135 - Hawa = Premium − Chips = 2319 − 1135 = 684 (approx) - Note: In bearish view, you SELL BOTH future AND option

Design — With Options (Intraday)

Best Times for Pure-Chips Options

  • Wednesday after 2:30 PM: Can get ITM with no hawa, 300-400 points below spot
  • Expiry day (Thursday) ~11 AM: Can get ITM with no hawa, 100-200 points below spot

Example

  • Buy BNF 7th APR 36700 CE @ 322 (acts as future, full chips)
  • Sell BNF MAY 35000 CE @ 2890
  • Market up → delta difference profit
  • Market crashes 600-700 points → weekly CE loses only premium, MAY CE rewards 600-700 points

Adjustment (If Trade Goes Against You)

Bullish Trade Reversal

  • Original: Buy MAY FUT + Sell MAY 35000 CE
  • Adjustment: Sell current month CE at resistance strike
  • Example: Sell BNF APR 37000 CE @ 1060
  • SL = Strike + Premium = 37000 + 1060 = 38060
  • If market crosses 38060 → SL hit on adjustment CE

Advantages of Hedge 23

  • Very reliable hedge
  • Exits cost-to-cost or minor loss even if wrong
  • ITM options behave like futures
  • Works in intraday AND positional
  • Can be done with futures OR weekly options

Important Rules

  1. Always build at next month expiry (more time, more hawa to capture)
  2. CE side is easier to adjust than PE side — prefer CE for bullish
  3. Start with positional first, then try intraday
  4. Start with Index first, then stock options
  5. Only enemy = VIX (sudden VIX rise adds premium to short position)
  6. Mark support/resistance on weekly chart with BB before entry
  7. Keep adjustment area and entry area clearly defined

Chips-Hawa Formula Summary

Chips (Call) = Future Price − Strike
Chips (Put)  = Strike − Future Price
Hawa         = Option Premium − Chips
Target       = Capture the Hawa
