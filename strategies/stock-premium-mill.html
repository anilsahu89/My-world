Stock Premium Mill — Monthly Hedged Covered Calls & Puts

Core Philosophy

> Same mill concept, different grindstone. Stocks don't have weekly expiry, so we collect premium MONTHLY using covered calls on shares you own, or cash-secured puts on stocks you want to own. Slower than Nifty, but you build an equity portfolio while collecting premium.

What This Strategy Is

A monthly stock option selling system that generates premium income from stocks you already hold (or want to hold). Two modes:

  1. Covered Call mode: You own 1000 shares of SBIN → sell monthly OTM call against them → collect premium. If called away, you sell at a profit. If not, you keep shares + premium.
  1. Cash-Secured Put mode: You want to buy Tata Motors at ₹900 (currently ₹950) → sell ₹900 put → collect ₹15 premium. If assigned, you buy at ₹885 (₹900 - ₹15). If not, keep premium.

This is NOT:

  • ❌ Naked option selling on random stocks
  • ❌ Day trading options
  • ❌ Buying options (you're the casino, not the gambler)

This IS:

  • ✅ Selling time value on stocks you understand
  • ✅ Monthly income from portfolio holdings
  • ✅ Getting paid to wait for entry prices (put selling)
  • ✅ Getting paid to exit at profit targets (call selling)

Key constraint: Stock options are monthly only. You can't do the "Wednesday → Thursday" theta game. The premium mill for stocks is one cycle per month, so each trade must be higher quality.

Strategy Design — Two Modes

MODE 1: Covered Call (You Own the Shares)

When to use: You hold shares of a good stock (fundamental pick) and want monthly income while waiting for appreciation.

Setup


You own:  1000 shares of SBIN (lot size 1500 for F&O, or any quantity for delivery selling)
Sell:     1 lot SBIN monthly OTM Call (strike = your sell target)
Collect:  Premium = yours to keep, regardless of outcome

Strike Selection

  • Sell strike = 5-8% above current price (your profit target)
  • If stock is at ₹500, sell ₹525-540 strike call
  • This means: "I'm happy to sell at ₹540. Pay me now for that promise."

Example: SBIN Covered Call


You own: 1500 SBIN shares @ ₹500 (₹7,50,000 value)
Sell:    1 lot SBIN 540 CE (monthly expiry) → collect ₹12/share = ₹18,000
Buy:     1 lot SBIN 480 PE (hedge) → pay ₹6/share = ₹9,000
Net credit: ₹3/share = ₹4,500 per month

Scenarios at expiry:
  SBIN < 480:  Shares worth ₹480+ = ₹7,20,000+ (put protects below 480)
               Premium kept: +₹4,500. Net position value: ₹7,24,500
  SBIN 480-540: Shares worth market price. Premium kept: +₹4,500
  SBIN > 540:  Shares sold at ₹540 = ₹8,10,000. Premium kept: +₹4,500
               Total: ₹8,14,500 (you made 8.6% + premium in one month)

Covered Call Rules

  1. Only sell calls against shares you're willing to sell at the strike price
  2. Strike = resistance level or 5-8% above current (whichever is closer)
  3. Sell at the start of monthly expiry (first week = max theta)
  4. Exit call at 50% profit if stock isn't moving toward strike
  5. If stock rallies past strike → let it be called away (you sold at your target)

MODE 2: Cash-Secured Put (You Want to Buy the Shares)

When to use: You want to buy a stock at a lower price, but don't want to wait. Get paid while waiting.

Setup


You have: Cash ready to buy (₹X reserved)
Sell:     1 lot monthly OTM Put at your buy target
Collect:  Premium = yours to keep
If assigned: You buy shares at strike - premium received (discount!)
If not assigned: You keep premium, try again next month

Strike Selection

  • Sell strike = strong support level or 10-15% below current price
  • Check weekly chart: sell at the lower Bollinger Band or previous major low
  • This is where HM oversold < 15 would be a buy zone

Example: Cash-Secured Put on Reliance


Reliance at ₹2,900. You want to buy at ₹2,600 (10% lower)
Sell: 1 lot Reliance 2600 PE (monthly) → collect ₹18/share = ₹10,800 (lot 600)
Hedge: Buy 1 lot Reliance 2400 PE → pay ₹5/share = ₹3,000
Net credit: ₹13/share = ₹7,800

Scenarios at expiry:
  Reliance > 2600: Put expires worthless. Keep ₹7,800. Try again next month.
  Reliance < 2600: Assigned. Buy 600 shares at ₹2600 = ₹15,60,000
                   Effective cost: ₹2,600 - ₹13 = ₹2,587/share (got paid to buy!)
  Reliance < 2400: Loss on hedge = limited. Shares worth ₹2,400+
                   Put protects below 2400.

Cash-Secured Put Rules

  1. Only sell puts on stocks you actually want to own (fundamental conviction)
  2. Have the full cash ready (don't sell puts on margin)
  3. Strike = support level (lower BB, previous low, 52-week low zone)
  4. If assigned → hold shares and switch to Covered Call mode (income on the position you now own)
  5. Never roll puts (don't keep selling to avoid assignment — if you get assigned, take the shares)

Stock Selection Criteria (Critical)

Eligible Stocks Only

  • ✅ F&O stocks only (they have liquid options with reasonable premiums)
  • ✅ Daily volume > 500,000 shares (liquidity = tight spreads)
  • ✅ Option premium > ₹5 at your target strike (otherwise not worth the effort)
  • ✅ Market cap > ₹10,000 crore (no micro-caps, no pump-and-dump risk)

VIX Check for Stocks

  • Check individual stock's IV (implied volatility), not just India VIX
  • Sell when stock IV is above its 30-day average (premium is "expensive" = good time to sell)
  • Avoid when IV is below 30-day average (premium is cheap = not worth the risk)

Direction Bias (from NK's system)

  • Stock above 20 SMA weekly + HM strength rising → sell puts (bullish, premium income)
  • Stock below 20 SMA weekly + HM strength falling → sell calls (bearish, if you own shares)
  • Stock at upper BB + HM > 86 → sell calls (overbought, expecting mean reversion)
  • Stock at lower BB + HM < 15 + curve forming → sell puts (oversold, accumulation zone)

The Monthly Routine (Stock Premium Mill)

WeekAction
Week 1 (Day after expiry)Scan F&O stocks for opportunities. Check which stocks are at BB extremes. Select 2-3 candidates. Enter trades.
Week 2Monitor. No action unless stock gaps significantly (earnings/event)
Week 3Check premiums. If at 50% profit → consider early exit
Week 4 (expiry week)Let deep OTM expire. Close anything near strike. Roll covered calls to next month if shares not called.

How Many Stocks to Run Simultaneously?

  • Start: 2-3 stocks (diversified sectors)
  • Target: 5-8 stocks (after 6 months of building)
  • Max: 10 stocks (don't over-diversify — monitoring becomes hard)

Sector Diversification

Never have all premium positions in one sector. Spread across:

  • Banking (SBIN, HDFC Bank)
  • IT (TCS, Infosys)
  • Energy (Reliance, ONGC)
  • FMCG (HUL, ITC)
  • Auto (Maruti, Tata Motors)

Capital Allocation

Covered Call Mode

  • Zero additional capital (you already own the shares)
  • Just sell calls against existing holdings
  • Premium = pure monthly income

Cash-Secured Put Mode

  • Reserve full notional value of the shares you might buy
  • Example: Selling 1 lot Reliance 2600 PE = reserve ₹15,60,000 (600 × 2600)
  • This is NOT margin — it's the cash to actually buy the shares if assigned
  • Max 30% of total capital deployed in put-selling at any time

Risk Management

Rule 1: Never Sell Naked

  • Every call sold must be covered by shares (covered call)
  • Every put sold must be covered by cash (cash-secured put)
  • NO naked option selling on stocks — gap risk is too high (earnings, FDA, regulatory)

Rule 2: Hedge with Far OTM Buy

  • Covered call → optionally buy a further OTM call (collar strategy)
  • Cash-secured put → always buy a far OTM put as hedge (defines max loss)
  • Cost of hedge: ₹2-8/share → reduces net premium but caps risk

Rule 3: Earnings Blackout

  • Never have open option positions during earnings week
  • Close or roll before earnings announcement
  • Earnings gaps can blow through any strike

Rule 4: SL on Cash-Secured Puts

  • If stock drops below your strike - hedge strike midpoint → close the position
  • Example: Sold 2600 PE, bought 2400 PE → midpoint = 2500
  • If stock closes below 2500 → exit (take the loss, don't hope for bounce)

Rule 5: No Averaging on Assigned Shares

  • If you get assigned on a put → hold the shares and sell covered calls
  • Do NOT sell more puts at lower strikes trying to "average down"
  • That's how you accumulate a falling knife

Why This Works for Fund Building

The Math (Covered Call example)


Stock: SBIN @ ₹500, lot 1500
Monthly call premium (540 CE): ₹12/share = ₹18,000
Annual income: ₹18,000 × 12 = ₹2,16,000
On ₹7,50,000 holding = 28.8% annual yield (premium alone)

Plus: If SBIN appreciates 10% = ₹50/share = ₹75,000
Total annual return: ~38-40% (premium + appreciation)

Even if stock is FLAT: 28.8% from premium alone

The Math (Cash-Secured Put example)


Stock: Reliance @ ₹2,900
Monthly put premium (2600 PE): ₹15/share = ₹9,000 (lot 600)
If NOT assigned (stock stays above 2600): pure ₹9,000 income = 0.58% monthly on reserved capital

If assigned 4 times out of 12 → you accumulate 2400 shares at avg ₹2,587
Then switch to covered calls on those 2400 shares → dual income stream
