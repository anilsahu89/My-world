Expiry Day Gap Hedge

Expiry Day Gap Hedge

Definition

Exploits the pricing gap between Bank Nifty Spot and Bank Nifty Future when the gap is ~200+ points. Buy near-expiry (weekly), sell far-expiry (monthly) — profit when gap converges at expiry.

Trigger Condition

  • When Spot-Future gap ≥ 200 points
  • Example: BN Spot = 39598, BN Future = 39834 → gap = 236 points
  • Per lot, this gap = ₹7,000–10,000 profit potential

Core Rule

> Always BUY near expiry (weekly), SELL far expiry (monthly).

The near-expiry option is calculated from spot, the far-expiry from future. At expiry, weekly matches spot → gap closes → profit.

Execution

Strike Selection

  • Select strike where market cannot reach by expiry
  • Near major resistance (e.g., 41000 if BN at 39598)
  • Or 42000/43000 if you think market could rise
  • Deeper OTM = safer — doesn't matter if premium is ₹1200 or ₹3000
  • Make it 1000-1500 points above current for near-zero probability of breach

Trade Structure

  • BUY Weekly PE (near expiry) — calculated from spot
  • SELL Monthly PE (far expiry) — calculated from future

Example

  • Weekly 41000 PE @ ₹1293
  • Monthly 41000 PE @ ₹1264
  • Don't trade if weekly > monthly — wait for weekly < monthly

When NOT to Trade

  • If weekly PE price > monthly PE price → wait
  • Wait for weekly price to drop below monthly (could be any day Mon-Thu)
  • Trade when: weekly price ≤ monthly price − 30-40 points

Profit Logic

  • Future expansion/erosion: 30-40 point difference still gives decent profit
  • Average profit: 180-200 points per trade
  • On expiry day or Wednesday → biggest profit

Risk Management

  • If market rises 41000+ → weekly becomes 0, monthly retains premium → adjustment needed
  • That's why we go 1000-1500 points deep — very low probability of breach
  • In downtrend → PE side; in uptrend → CE side

CE vs PE Side

Market TrendSide
DowntrendPE side (put hedge)
UptrendCE side (call hedge)

Key Rules

  1. Gap trigger: ≥200 points between spot and future
  2. Direction: Buy near expiry, sell far expiry
  3. Strike: 1000-1500 points OTM (deep, safe)
  4. Pricing check: Weekly price must be < monthly price
  5. Best timing: Expiry day or Wednesday
  6. If Friday/Monday setup: Weekly must be 30-40 points cheaper than monthly
